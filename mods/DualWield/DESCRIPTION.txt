Fight with a weapon in each hand. Pairs of one-handed axes, knives, maces and swords are supported. Two knives fight like the game's Skoll and Hati; two axes, two maces or two swords swing like the Berserkir axes.

Equip a one-handed axe, knife, mace or sword, then equip a second one of the same kind: it goes into your off hand, replacing your shield or torch. You need 20 in that weapon skill (Clubs for maces); for axes, half of your Woodcutting counts too (Axes + half of Woodcutting must reach 20). Below it you are told how your total is made up. Hold Left Alt while equipping to replace your main weapon instead. Putting away the main weapon moves the off-hand one across. Equipping a shield, torch or another kind of weapon ends dual wielding as usual.

Balance: each hit is worth 62% of both weapons' damage combined, about 1.24× one weapon when they are equal, normalized against the dual move set's own combo. Every swing costs 1.3× the main weapon's stamina. Both weapons wear down. Blocking uses only the main weapon's weak block, shown with both weapons raised, so a shield is still the defensive choice. The skill you train is the pair's own. Mixed pairs are not supported yet.

Install for every player: the off-hand weapon and the dual stance are seen by others through the game's own equipment and animation sync, and the off-hand position needs this mod to look right. The damage share, stamina, skill requirement, swap key, and each kind's off-hand position and rotation can be changed in F7. Compiled and checked against the local game; the off-hand look, move sets, balance and GearSlots interaction still need in-game playtests.
