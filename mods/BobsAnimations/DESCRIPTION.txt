An animation pack for other mods: keyframed clips played over a character's own animation, a bone at a time, so a villager sitting in a chair keeps sitting while their arm lifts a tankard to their lips. No asset bundles: clips are made in Blender over a skeleton captured from the game and shipped as small files inside the mod.

Seated clips: sit_drink (a long pull from a tankard), sit_toast_left/right (raised toward someone, then a pull), sit_talk_left/right (open-handed beats toward a neighbour, head turned, jaw working), sit_point_left/right, sit_laugh (slapping the knee, doubled over), sit_think (a fist under the chin, stroking the beard), sit_cheer (both fists pumped), sit_board_move (a piece moved on the table in front), and two loops, sit_fold_arms and sit_elbows (leaning forward, elbows on the knees). A seated clip blends out by itself when its character gets up.

Standing clips: stand_fish (the rod held out, a loop), stand_fish_cast, stand_fish_reel (a fish taken off the line and held up), walk_carry (an armful carried while walking, the item given when played), stand_sow and stand_harvest (crouched, feet planted), stand_watch (a hand over the eyes, looking about), stand_drum (a loop) and stand_recite. A standing clip ends by itself when its character walks off; one clip after another blends without a snap.

For modders: Anim.Play(character, "sit_drink"), Anim.Play(character, "walk_carry", "Wood"), Anim.Stop(character), Anim.Playing(character); Clip.Register(json) adds your own clips. The game that runs a character starts and stops its clip; what plays and since when is kept on the character's network object, so everyone near sees the same, in step. A clip blends in and out over the game's pose, and can put an item (a tankard) in a hand while it plays.

Install it on every player who should see the animations (and on the game that runs the characters). Native Linux and Windows.
