An animation pack for other mods: keyframed clips played over a character's own animation, a bone at a time, so a villager sitting in a chair keeps sitting while their arm lifts a tankard to their lips. No asset bundles: clips are made in Blender over a skeleton captured from the game and shipped as small files inside the mod.

For now it has one clip, sit_drink: seated, a tankard in the right hand, lifted off the thigh, a long pull with the head going back, lowered, and set back down. More seated clips are planned (toast, talk, point, laugh, think, lean on the table, a board game move).

For modders: Anim.Play(character, "sit_drink"), Anim.Stop(character), Anim.Playing(character); Clip.Register(json) adds your own clips. The game that runs a character starts and stops its clip; what plays and since when is kept on the character's network object, so everyone near sees the same, in step. A clip blends in and out over the game's pose, and can put an item (a tankard) in a hand while it plays.

Install it on every player who should see the animations (and on the game that runs the characters). Native Linux and Windows.
