0.3.2: Fingers bend the right way. Every grip and gesture curled fingers backward (away from the palm), and began from the captured hand (curled over a knee) rather than straight fingers; every clip is remade: open hands talk with a relaxed curl, fists close, the pointing index stays straight with the rest folded in, the tankard's handle is gripped.

0.3.1: An armful carried while walking stays in the arms: a prop hangs from the hand itself where its attach point rests (the game's walk turns the attach point in the hand, which swung the load about), and arms set by a clip hang from shoulders the clip sets too (walking swings the shoulders). The carried load sits between the hands. Claude Tools: anim drift (each playing clip's weight, the hand's and prop's place in the character's frame, and whether anything moved the bones after this mod), for characters out of the player's view, whose skin a shot can't show as it is.

0.3.0: The standing set: stand_fish (the rod held out, a loop), stand_fish_cast and stand_fish_reel (a fish taken off the line and held up), walk_carry (an armful carried while walking; the item is given when it's played), stand_sow and stand_harvest (crouched, the feet kept planted), stand_watch (a hand over the eyes, looking about), stand_drum (a loop, the drum under the left arm) and stand_recite. Clips can move the hips (crouching), blend from where the clip before left the body (one after another without a snap), and a still clip ends by itself when its character walks off. Api Version 2: PlayWith(character, clip, item). Claude Tools: anim play ... item=<prefab>; anim chars says who stands still.

0.2.1: BobsAnimations.Api (Version 1) for other mods, found by reflection as Jarldom finds its neighbours: Play, Stop, Playing, Has, Length. Jarldom 0.1.63 uses it for its seated villagers.

0.2.0: The seated set: sit_toast_left/right (the tankard raised toward someone, then a pull), sit_talk_left/right (open-handed beats toward a neighbour, the head turned to them, the jaw working), sit_point_left/right, sit_laugh (back, slapping the knee, doubled over), sit_think (a fist under the chin, stroking the beard), sit_cheer (both fists pumped), sit_board_move (a piece moved on the table in front), and two loops: sit_fold_arms and sit_elbows. A clip marked seated (all of these) blends out by itself when its character gets up, and the game that runs the character ends it for everyone. Stop clears a clip no game here knows. Claude Tools: anim near (pieces round a character, in its own frame) and anim params (its animator's switches). Authoring: mirrored clips, finger-by-finger grips.

0.1.0: First version, a prototype. Clips played over a character's own animation (spine, neck and head turned from the game's pose; arms and fingers set), blended in and out, kept on the character's network object so every game near plays them in step, with props held in a hand while they play. One clip: sit_drink (seated, a tankard to the lips and back down), tried in game on seated Jarldom villagers, men and women. An "anim" command for Claude Tools (chars, clips, rig, hold, play, stop, shot), and the Blender authoring scripts in Authoring/.
