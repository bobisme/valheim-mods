A navigator's kit for the northern seas: plotted courses, a sun-compass and sunstone, a sounding line, a weather-foretelling primstav, Flóki's ravens, ship names and fittings, beacon fires, sea-marks and a weather vane.

Plotted course: open the big map and hold Ctrl. Click to lay a waypoint. Click on a pin and the waypoint takes the pin's name; click on a leg and it goes into that leg. Ctrl+right-click lifts a waypoint, Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+R turns the course round for the way home, and Ctrl+Backspace clears it. The course is drawn on both maps as a dashed line, with a fainter line from you to the next waypoint. Under the compass, the navigator's notes give the next waypoint's distance and bearing, the distance left and the time left at your speed, and the wind for this leg: fair, on the quarter, on the beam, or a headwind, with the sail's pull as the game reckons it. Reaching a waypoint (40 m) moves on to the next, and cutting a corner to a later one counts. Each character keeps one course per world. Writing your map at a cartography table leaves your course there too, and reading the table takes it. While you steer, everyone aboard sees your course in blue.

Sun-Compass (workbench: 2 fine wood, 2 resin): a notched bearing-dial like the Uunartoq disc. Carry it and a compass band runs across the top of the screen, with your heading and marks for the next waypoint, home (your bed) and your raven's flight. It reads the sun by day and the lodestar by night. Cloud, rain and mist hide the sun, and the band drifts into dead reckoning.
Sunstone (workbench: 1 crystal, 1 leather scraps): a sólarsteinn of clear spar. It finds the sun through cloud and mist, so the compass stays true by day in any weather. It does not help at night.
Sounding Line (workbench: 2 stone, 4 leather scraps): aboard a ship it is cast ahead of the bow every few seconds. You get the depth in fathoms where she will be, what the bottom is (sand, mud, rock, shell), a shout when the water shoals, and a note when you are over the deep sea, where the serpents are.
Primstav (workbench: 1 fine wood, 1 coal): the game draws its weather from the clock, so the stave tells truly what comes next and when: "Clear skies; rain in 3:20". When the next waypoint lies in another land, it also gives that land's weather. A raid, a boss or Moder's power can still bring its own weather.
Caged Raven (workbench: 2 wood, 3 feathers, 1 raw meat): use it to let the raven go. It climbs, circles, and flies for the nearest land it sees, unexplored land first. Your map keeps a line the way it flew, and the land where it was heading is drawn in. If it sees no land within 2 km it comes back to you. Only you see your raven.

Ships: every ship has a name, a kenning or a famous ship's name until you give her your own at the helm with Shift+E. Each ship you have sailed in is pinned on your map by name, so you never lose your boat. Taking the pin off the map forgets her. Masts carry a lantern, lit from dusk till dawn, and a gilded vane at the masthead that swings to the wind. Shift+E at the mast climbs to the lookout's seat, a board lashed under the masthead: up there the map fills in wider around you, the higher the seat the wider (three times as wide from a drakkar's), and a spyglass reaches farther.

Building (hammer): Weather Vane (1 bronze, 2 fine wood), a gilded veðurviti on a pole that swings to the wind; look at it for the wind and the weather to come. Beacon Fire (12 wood, 12 stone, 6 resin), the game's bonfire lifted onto a timber tower with an iron basket. Fed with wood, it burns with a glow seen from far out at sea. Shift+E names it, and everyone who has seen it keeps it on their map. Sea-Mark (4 wood, 2 resin), a floating spar-buoy with a birch-withy topmark and a little lamp that burns at night. Place it on water; name it (E) and it goes on your map.

Everything is in F7 → Mod settings. Players need the mod to see the course, compass and notes. The host and nearby players need it for the built pieces, items on the ground, ship names and the helm's course; a host without it deletes those objects. Removing the mod removes its items from inventories.
