It now lands you safely, on the deck found under the mast (not the mast seat's height, which on the karve was above the yard). Up at the masthead you count as aboard, so the wind and lead notes stay. No more error from looking up the world while none is loaded.

The helmsman's whole course (up to 64 waypoints with names) rode on the ship, which is resent to everyone nearby with every move, and stayed on it after they left the helm; now only the leg being sailed and the next few go aboard, and they come off when the helmsman lets go. 0.1.0: First Wayfinder. Plotted courses on the map (Ctrl+click), drawn on both maps and followed leg by leg, shared through cartography tables and with everyone aboard. Navigator's notes: next waypoint, distance, time left, wind for the leg, sounding and forecast. Items: Sun-Compass (compass band by sun and lodestar), Sunstone (through cloud), Sounding Line (depth in fathoms, bottom, shoal and serpent-water warnings), Primstav (exact forecast from the game's own weather clock), Caged Raven (flies for the nearest land and marks the way). Ships: names (Shift+E at the helm), map pins for ships you have sailed, mast lanterns by night, gilded masthead vanes, a masthead to climb (wider map reveal). Pieces: Weather Vane, Beacon Fire (seen from afar, pinned for those who saw it), floating Sea-Mark.
