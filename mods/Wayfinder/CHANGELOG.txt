0.1.8: No more stutter every two seconds in a big base: beacons and sea-marks are kept in a list as they come and go, instead of the whole scene being searched for them (20–25 ms).

0.1.7: Test tool only: `wayfinder remove` can be given ship or piece names, to take away only those test objects.

0.1.6: Sight from the masthead scales with the ship: MastheadSight is now the bonus from the tallest seat, a drakkar's (16 m over the sea), and lower seats get less as the horizon draws in (a karve's about 2.3x, a longship's about 2.5x, of the default 3x). On a ship rolling at sea you sit on the lookout's seat, not a foot above it: the mast is measured in the ship's own frame. Tried on the longship and the drakkar.

0.1.5: The longship's and drakkar's pegs no longer run down through the hull and under the sea: the mast is measured from its own shown parts only (they carry a hidden sail under the mast reaching far below the keel). Checked on the karve, longship and drakkar.

0.1.4: Up at the masthead you sit on the lookout's seat, not above it: your sitting height is measured the first time you sit there and the seat point follows. No fall damage going up or coming down. The pegs, lantern, vane and seat stay on the mast when the yard swings round (they were placed from the middle of the turning mast and sail).

0.1.3: A lookout's seat on every mast: a board lashed to the fore side under the masthead, slung by two ropes, with pegs up the mast. Climbing to the masthead now sits you on it, not in the air beside the mast; everyone aboard sees the seat.

0.1.2: Fixed a deadly fall from the masthead: climbing down (a key, or a reload) set you on the deck, but the game counted the drop from the masthead as a fall. It now lands you safely, on the deck found under the mast (not the mast seat's height, which on the karve was above the yard). Up at the masthead you count as aboard, so the wind and lead notes stay. No more error from looking up the world while none is loaded.

0.1.1: Less network traffic at sea. The helmsman's whole course (up to 64 waypoints with names) rode on the ship, which is resent to everyone nearby with every move, and stayed on it after they left the helm; now only the leg being sailed and the next few go aboard, and they come off when the helmsman lets go. The course is asked for again every 2 s until it lands, not every frame.

0.1.0: First Wayfinder. Plotted courses on the map (Ctrl+click), drawn on both maps and followed leg by leg, shared through cartography tables and with everyone aboard. Navigator's notes: next waypoint, distance, time left, wind for the leg, sounding and forecast. Items: Sun-Compass (compass band by sun and lodestar), Sunstone (through cloud), Sounding Line (depth in fathoms, bottom, shoal and serpent-water warnings), Primstav (exact forecast from the game's own weather clock), Caged Raven (flies for the nearest land and marks the way). Ships: names (Shift+E at the helm), map pins for ships you have sailed, mast lanterns by night, gilded masthead vanes, a masthead to climb (wider map reveal). Pieces: Weather Vane, Beacon Fire (seen from afar, pinned for those who saw it), floating Sea-Mark.
