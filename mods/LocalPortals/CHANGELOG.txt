0.1.4: A mirror between the camera and you no longer vanishes: its wood turns to a faint see-through glow in its colour, with its inlay, gem and motes as they were, so you see yourself through it and can still tell it is there.
0.1.3: Walking into a mirror against a wall works: low wall pieces behind it (from a hand above the floor) let you through too, and your shoulders can pass the edges of the frame while you are lined up with the opening (you are nearly as wide as it, so being a little off centre used to stop you).
0.1.2: A mirror can stand right against a wall: walls just behind its glass no longer stop you before you are through (you had to get your middle past the glass, and the wall held you back). Floors and the ground still hold you up.
0.1.1: Nothing of you pokes out of the back of either mirror while you are partway through: your body is cut off at the glass you are entering, and the double coming out of the other mirror is cut off at its glass, so from behind both mirrors show only their wooden backs.
