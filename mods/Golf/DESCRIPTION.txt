Meadow Golf: build a little Viking golf course out of the landscape and your own structures. Craft a carved wooden golf club at a workbench (6 wood, 2 leather scraps). Hammer → Furniture supplies golf tees (2 wood) and cups with linen flags (4 wood, 1 stone).

Shift+E labels a tee or cup as Course:Hole:Par, for example Meadow:1:3. Give both ends the same course name and hole number; the tee sets par. The builder can edit labels. Courses have up to 18 holes, par 2–8; use a unique number for each cup. A matching cup must be loaded and within 240 m when you start.

Equip the club and E at a tee to set your colored wooden ball. Stand within 3 m of the stationary ball, aim with the camera, use the wheel to choose Drive / Chip / Putt, then hold left mouse to charge and release to swing. Gentle putts begin at low power; charging takes 1.4 seconds. An amber path and stopping-point ring predict flight, bounces and rolling with the actual ball physics. The HUD distinguishes predicted range (with its sampled power) from distance to the cup. Right click cancels charging. The native swing contact event launches the ball, with a wooden impact and quieter bounce sounds. Prediction is spread across frames; unreliable results show a short direction line. Balls roll, bounce and hit real terrain and buildings; they do not damage anything. Players and creatures pass through balls, and golfers cannot hit someone else's ball. A slow ball entering its linked cup finishes the hole.

G opens the scorecard for nearby golfers (120 m), with finished-hole strokes, par and totals. Your current course card and ball save in the world. A replay replaces that hole's result; changing courses starts a new card. Ctrl+E at a tee starts a fresh round. E on your ball returns it to the last shot's lie for one penalty; E on the same unfinished hole's tee returns it to the tee for one penalty. There is a 99-stroke ceiling; Ctrl+E begins again. Cup rings are visual and never excavate terrain. Indoor and basement courses use actual floors, rather than lifting the ball to outdoor terrain height.

Install Meadow Golf on the host and every player, then restart before loading a world: the club, tees, cups and balls are new saved prefabs. Mouse/keyboard controls; G is adjustable in F7. No dependency on BuildOrders or Gary. Reloads cancel a charged swing and reattach Golf's scripts to saved pieces/balls. No periodic whole-scene scans; the host indexes saved golf balls once per world/reload in batches.

0.1.0 is compiled on Linux and checked against the installed native game, with tests for marker validation, shot limits, cup capture, scoring, fresh rounds and replay/penalty accounting. Creative playtests passed for shots, cup completion, scorecards, native swing contact/audio and Golf-only reloads. Live hall ricochets and disposable floor, ramp and basement-ceiling fixtures matched predicted endpoints. Multiplayer ownership, disconnects and latency still need a multi-player test. Optional ClaudeTools commands: golf status, golf shot drive|chip|putt <power> <dx> <dz>. Testcourse/testclear only operate on locally hosted Creative and do not change terrain or existing buildings.
