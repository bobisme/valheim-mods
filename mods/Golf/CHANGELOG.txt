0.1.0: First Meadow Golf release. Carved wooden club, painted player balls, hammer-placeable tees and flagged cups. Linked course/hole labels; charged drive/chip/putt shots with native animation contact, swing/impact/bounce sounds and right-click charge cancellation. Real surface physics and a private, frame-budgeted physics preview of flight, ricochets and settling, with a stopping-point ring and separately labeled cup distance. Nearby scorecards, saved rounds, replay/new-round controls and one-stroke lie/tee penalties. Native owner physics with kinematic replicas; players/creatures/other balls do not block shots. Linux build and native API checks, scoring/rule tests and Creative playtests including floors, ramps and basement ceilings. Multiplayer latency and disconnect/ownership transfer remain untested.
