Livestock for the long houses: sheep in the Meadows and goats in the Mountains, to tame, breed, shear and milk.

Wild herds: sheep graze in small herds (two to four) on open Meadows away from the trees, goats on steep Mountain slopes. They're shy: they run from you when you come within 8 m (goats 10 m), half that if you crouch. Hurt one and it runs. They never fight back.

Taming: as with boars. Drop what they eat and stay back; a fed animal calms and tames in time (sheep about 20 minutes, goats 30). Sheep eat dandelions, berries, carrots, turnips, barley and cloudberries; goats all that and thistle and onions too.

Breeding: a fed, tame pair has young (lambs and kids) now and then, up to six of a kind close together. The young grow up in under two days and come tame.

Shears (forge, 3 copper): carry them and press E on a tame sheep in full fleece. It drops 2 or 3 wool, 1 if it was hungry. The wool grows back in two days, twice as long if it's kept hungry. Look at a sheep to see how long it has to go.

Milk pail (workbench, 6 wood): carry it and press E on a tame goat. It gives a jug or two of goat's milk once a day (one, and every two days, if hungry). Goat's milk is food: a little health, good stamina.

Feeding trough (workbench, 10 wood): put food in it, and hungry tame animals within 12 m walk over and eat from it, one bite at a time, each its own food. Boars, wolves and the other tames use it too.

Hay rack (workbench, 12 wood): a slatted crib on legs, six slots, fed from like the trough. Stall divider (workbench, 6 wood): a plank partition 2 m long and chest high that snaps end to end, to walls and to floors; rows of them off a wall make stalls, closed with the game's fence gate.

With Jarldom: a herder shears and milks the colony's animals in a pasture, with shears or a pail from the stores, and carries the wool and milk home. A Byre room (roofed, with a trough or hay rack; stalls and gates grade it up) warms a longhouse that shares a wall with it.

Settings (decided by the server in multiplayer): wild herds on or off, how often they come, how fast wool and milk come back, and what the shears, pail, trough, hay rack and stall divider cost.

Needs a restart to install or update: the animals, items and pieces are saved in the world. Every player and the server need it.
