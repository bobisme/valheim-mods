0.3.0: cattle, pigs and geese (milestone S2). Cattle: a small red-and-white horned breed in the Meadows, milked with the pail (cow's milk), a big hide when slaughtered, led on a halter; Byre.Api 3's CanPull is true for a tame grown cow. Geese: on the game's hen, by the water in the Meadows; plucked by hand for feathers; a tame goose lays eggs that hatch goslings under a roof by a fire (or two cooked eggs each at the cauldron), and chases strangers (wild creatures, players who haven't petted it) with a honk, stopping short: no harm. Pigs: a tame boar's litter is now and then (25%) pig piglets, which breed true; a tame pig led through the Black Forest roots up truffles (about one in ten minutes). New settings: CowChance, GooseChance, PigFromBoarChance, TruffleMinutes. Live-checked: a cow milked, a goose plucked, eggs laid and one hatched, pig piglets from tame boars, a truffle rooted up in the Black Forest, a goose chasing a stranger and stopping short then leaving him be once petted. Not yet seen: wild cattle and geese spawning and taming, the truffle rate over time, the cauldron recipe, young growing up, multiplayer.

0.2.0: a hay rack and a stall divider, and Byre.Api 2 for villagers. The hay rack (workbench, 12 wood) is a slatted crib on legs, six slots, fed from like the trough; the stall divider (workbench, 6 wood) is a chest-high plank partition 2 m long that snaps end to end and to walls, for rows of stalls closed with the game's fence gate. Byre.Api 2: each kind's product and tool, an animal's kind, whether it's young, the time left, the herds and feeders near a place, and a harvest call (Jarldom's herders shear and milk with it). Live-checked: both pieces placed and the rack taken for a feeder; a Jarldom herder sheared a sheep and milked a goat through the API. Not yet tried by a player: placing and snapping the stall divider with the hammer.

Test commands: byre bait (food at a wild one's feet), byre troughs fill, taming time left in byre herds.
