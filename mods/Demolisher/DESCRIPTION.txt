Take down a whole building in one go, with Thor's lightning striking it as it falls. Pick the Demolition stake from the hammer's Misc tab, click the ground for each corner of the area, click the first corner again (or press E) to close it, and everything inside that the hammer could remove turns red. Press E: "Demolish N structures?" Yes calls down the Obliterator's lightning: a rumble of thunder and sparks, then the bolts strike (harmless: nobody and nothing outside the outline is hurt) and everything comes down top first, workshops last, a few each frame, by the hammer's own rules: materials drop on the ground, wards, no-build zones and chests with items are left standing, and pieces needing a crafting station need it near you (as with the hammer). Only pieces built by players are touched, never ships or carts.

Backspace removes the last corner (or reopens a closed outline), Shift+Backspace clears it. The stake itself is never built. Only the player demolishing needs the mod; removals go through the game's own network calls. Native Linux and Windows.
