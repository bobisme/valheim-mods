0.1.3: Where the road meets the village: Ctrl+click with the stake on the site's edge for egress points. With two, the road runs through the village, its houses along it and its square beside it; with one, the village's way out goes there, and a Ctrl+click well outside the site adds the extension point on the road that a lane runs on to. Ctrl+Backspace removes the last. In a world with Fimbulwinter Roads the points snap onto the road, and a village between two points of one road follows the road itself and leaves its ground alone. Fires and torches are built full of fuel.
0.1.2: Building takes down old constructions in the village's way (world ruins, and others unless Clear old buildings is off; never a chest with something in it), and Take away puts them back. Signs by the doors of the smithy, storehouses and boathouse. Tables and beds keep clear of a longhouse's hearths. Longhouse smoke holes are open (Hallwright 0.11.3).
0.1.1: A palisade always has a gate: a street that stopped short of the ring (a hill fort's often did) is carried on through it.
0.1.0: First version. The Villagewright stake lays out a whole village in a circle or a drawn plot: a square, streets along the gentlest ground, a hall and temple on the square, houses along the streets (Hallwright longhouses and timber halls, furnished), a smithy, storehouses, a boathouse and dock by water, fields and kitchen gardens, a palisade with gates, torches. Six kinds of village. Build village puts it all up in creative play, with the ground levelled and painted; Take away last village removes it and puts the ground back.
