Lay out a whole Viking village on the land and build it. Pick the Villagewright stake in the hammer's build menu (Misc), click the ground for the village's centre and again for its edge (a circle 48 to 320 m across), or Shift+click round a plot, corner by corner. Press E (or L) for the settings.

The settings: what kind of village (Hamlet, Farming, Fishing, Market, Hill fort, Temple town), how many houses, fields and storehouses, how many ways out, intricacy and street width, and switches for the chieftain's hall, temple, smithy, market, palisade, dock and torches. Another layout tries a new one; the same layout number on the same land always gives the same village.

How it's laid out: the square goes on the flattest ground near the middle (on high ground for a hill fort, near the water for a fishing village). Streets run from it to the ways out along the gentlest slopes, and down to the shore for a dock. The hall and temple face the square; houses line the streets with their doors to them, each turned a little its own way, and lanes branch off when the streets fill up. The smithy goes out towards a gate, storehouses by the fields, a boathouse by the water. Fields lie beyond the houses, fenced, rows of crops along the nearest street; about half the houses have a fenced kitchen garden behind. A palisade rings it all with gaps where the streets leave. Houses are Norse longhouses (dug in, long hearth, sleeping platforms) or timber halls, the temple a stave temple, all designed by Hallwright's geometry. They're furnished (beds on the platforms or against the walls, chests, a table and benches, a rug), with woodpiles, barrels and benches in their yards. The square has a fire with benches round it (and market stalls, or a maypole).

Build village (creative play: devcommands with nocost): trees, rocks and bushes in the village are cleared, the ground is levelled under every building and the square, streets are smoothed along their length and paved or trodden, fields tilled, and every piece is put down as your own building. Take away last village removes every piece it put down and puts the ground back (where nothing else has changed it since); felled trees stay felled. The record is per world in BepInEx/config/villagewright-<world>.json.

The layout engine is Fimbulwinter's (it will make that mod's towns from the world's seed); this mod is the tool to try it anywhere.

Claude Tools: village scout [range] [radius] [coast] finds open, dry Meadows away from anything built; village site <radius> [x z] / plot <x,z>... marks the site; village options kind=... seed=N|new houses=N hall=on|off ...; village plan / status / design / build / undo / list / window / preview on|off.
