Bob's Pipes: a reusable carved corewood pipe, made at the workbench. Prepare Meadow, Honeywood or Cloudberry pipe tobacco at Quad's Cigar Rolling Table. Use a tin to pack an empty pipe, then use your hotbar pipe near a burning fire or holding a torch to light it. Use it again to put it out and save the unfinished bowl. The lit pipe rests in your mouth, with a small ember, quiet smoke, occasional exhalations and a slowly seasoned finish. Combat, swimming and exposed rain put it away. One active smoke, with small blend bonuses configurable down to cosmetic only. Requires Quad's Cigars smoking API v1 (0.2.1).
