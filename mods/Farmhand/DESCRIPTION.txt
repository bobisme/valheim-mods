Lay out a whole crop plot with your cultivator: select a crop, then Shift+click corners in boundary order. Concave outlines work. Shift+J plants the previewed layout as you walk through the field, within normal cultivator reach. The first edge sets row direction; spacing leaves a half-cell border and increases for larger growth radii. Green dots are within reach; amber dots wait until you approach; blocked spots turn red.

Escape pauses, and Shift+J resumes without repeating successful spots. Recover stamina automatically; add seeds or repair the tool when paused. Backspace removes the last corner; Delete clears the plot. At most 32 corners, 512 spots and 400 square metres by default. Self-crossing outlines are rejected. Plot previews and progress clear on reload or changing characters.

Without a marked plot, Shift+J still plants a short row. Shift+U harvests and replants nearby mature crops; add Left Ctrl to harvest only. Unhealthy crops get helpful labels.

Every successful planting uses ordinary seeds/materials, stamina and cultivator durability. Cultivation, biome, sunlight, space, obstacles and wards still apply. Harvest drops remain world items. BuildFromChests can supply seeds. Select a crop or seed crop, not a tree or vine; turn off BuildOrders plan mode. No extra mod is required on the host or peers.
