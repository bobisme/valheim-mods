Grave goods: the weapons and war-gear the dead were buried with. Treasure chests in crypts, caves, barrows, towers and camps (and buried treasure and shipwrecks) sometimes hold one: a real weapon, armour piece or shield, from the depth of the world the chest lies in, now and then one deeper or shallower.

Five rarities, each rarer than the last: Plain (white: just the thing, buried), Fine (green: one power), Runed (blue: two), Heirloom (purple: three, and its own name, a kenning such as Wound-wand or Battle-moon), and Saga (orange: a weapon or piece of gear from the sagas, such as Gram, Tyrfing, Skofnung, Gunnar's Atgeir, Svalinn or the Helm of Awe, with its own powers and lore and two more of chance). Rarer finds are better kept (higher quality). With the default settings, about one chest in three holds grave goods; a Runed find is about one chest in sixteen, an Heirloom one in forty-four, and a Saga find one in a hundred and sixty. Each saga item turns up once in a world until every one of its kind has been found.

Powers: weapons can hit harder, deal part of their damage as fire, frost, lightning, poison or spirit, cost less stamina to swing, stagger more, stab harder from behind, drink blood (heal you by a share of what they deal) or raise their own skill. Armour and shields can add armour or block power, health, stamina or eitr, faster health or stamina regeneration, movement speed, carry weight, a skill, or a ward against fire, frost, poison, lightning or spirit (wards add up to at most 80%). Any of them can be dverg-forged (more durability) or feather-light. Powers work while the thing is worn or wielded; their numbers show in the game's own tooltip lines (damage, armour, block, durability, weight), and the powers are listed in the rarity's colour at the top of the tooltip. Grave goods' slots are tinted in their colour, runed and rarer finds glow where they lie, and opening a chest on a find names it.

Every chest is rolled once, the first time it loads near someone, chests found before the mod too. The chance and the rarity weights are in F7 → Mod settings. Grave goods are ordinary items with a record on them: without the mod they are plain weapons and armour again, and nothing is lost. Each player needs the mod for the powers to work for them; whoever first loads a chest needs it for the chest to be rolled.
