Treasure chests hold buried weapons, armour and shields of five rarities (Plain, Fine, Runed, Heirloom, Saga) with powers: damage, elements, stamina, stagger, sneak attacks, blood-drinking, skills, armour, block, health, stamina, eitr, regeneration, speed, carry weight, wards, durability and weight. Twenty saga items with their lore, each found once a world. Coloured names, powers at the top of tooltips, tinted slots, a glow on the ground, and a message when a chest holds something rare.
