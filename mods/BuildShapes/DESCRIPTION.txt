Draw curves, arches, arcs and straight runs of beams, mirror pieces and repeat decorations as shared BuildOrders ghosts. Requires BuildOrders with its planning and ghost-selection APIs. Hallwright (timber halls, longhouses, stave temples) is its own mod since BuildShapes 0.9.0: install Hallwright for the stake.

Pick a piece in the hammer as usual, then hold Left Ctrl (about a third of a second, without clicking or scrolling) for the shape radial: flick towards Curve, Arch, Arc, Line, Mirror or Repeat and let go (or click). It looks like the game's own radial; shapes that need something you haven't got are greyed out and say what, and Build leaves the shape tool. Escape, or letting go in the middle, closes it.

In a shape tool: left click places markers; once they're all down, the real pieces show. Scroll turns them (in the game's 22.5° steps), E plans them as shared ghosts (and uses nothing you're looking at), Delete clears the shape and keeps the tool, Backspace removes the last marker, U removes your last shape's unbuilt ghosts, and Escape leaves the tool. Ctrl+scroll sets Arch's and Arc's height and Repeat's spacing (Shift with it for fine steps). Ctrl+click selects pieces in Mirror and copies one in Repeat; a slow Ctrl+click or Ctrl+scroll that opens the radial still does what it would have done.

Curve: select a beam/pole and click start, bend, end. Native-length beams follow the curve in 3D with overlapping joints. Requires two endpoint snap points and a span of 0.25–8 metres.

Arch: select a beam/pole and click both ends. The arch rises straight up from them: an upright leg one beam long at each end (three quarters of the height on a low arch), so the end beams stand plumb, and a half-ellipse between the legs' tops, a semicircle when it's half as high as it's wide. Ctrl+scroll sets the height above the ends' middle (0–32 m); a gold guide marks the top. Unequal end heights work; the ends must be apart horizontally.

Arc: like Arch, but the circle through both ends and the top: its ends lean as the circle has them (a half-circle stands up at its ends).

Line: select a beam/pole and click start and end. Native-length beams run straight between the markers at any slope, or upright; the outer beams meet both markers and the overlaps are spread evenly.

Mirror: click two points for a vertical mirror plane, then Ctrl+click built pieces or visible ghosts to toggle source selection. Thin wire boxes show sources; the mirrored copies show as real pieces. Ctrl+Backspace removes the last selection. Reflection axes and pivot compensation follow native snap layouts, fixing triangular under-roof walls and sloped beams while preserving ordinary roof pitch; mesh bounds are a fallback. Asymmetric carvings and handed meshes keep their original geometry, so inspect the ghosts before building.

Repeat: click start, bend, end with any normal building piece selected. Scroll turns each piece, Ctrl+scroll sets the spacing, Home switches turning with the curve, and Ctrl+click copies a built piece or ghost and its orientation. O opens the full options: path anchor (piece origin, visual center, or a native snap point), spacing, yaw, pitch and roll, with exact values (type, then Enter or Apply, or use the ± buttons; Shift for finer steps). E plans from there too; O or Escape closes it. Spacing fits evenly to both endpoints; the chosen anchor stays on the path after rotation, marked by gold crosses.

Live draft sharing: other players with BuildShapes see your markers and shape previews as cyan visuals labeled with your name. Sharing is on by default; F7 exposes send/receive and piece-preview toggles under Shared drafts. These visuals have no colliders, costs or build actions; E still creates normal shared BuildOrders ghosts. Drafts clear on submission/exit/unload and expire after disconnects. Viewers draw the nearest four drafts within 120 m, with at most 4,096 shared pieces visible.

Previews are the real pieces, local only (no colliders, no cost). Curves are limited to 128 metres and every shape to 256 pieces. Markers/sources require 40-metre reach; the planner checks all output positions, recipes, wards, and no-build areas. Scaled and terrain-operation pieces are excluded. Selections are pose snapshots.

Shapes never change terrain; built pieces stay intact and normal materials/support still apply. Undo is one submitted shape across all tools and clears on death, world change, or F6. Only the designer needs BuildShapes; participating players should use updated BuildOrders and the selected pieces. Keys are in F7 (Controls): ShapeMenu (Left Ctrl), ConfirmShape (E), RepeatOptions (O). ClaudeTools: shapes radial [wedge|off] holds the radial open for pictures.
