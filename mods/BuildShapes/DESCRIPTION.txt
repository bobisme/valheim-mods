Draw curves, mirror building groups, and repeat decorations along a curve as shared BuildOrders ghosts. Requires BuildOrders with its planning and ghost-selection APIs.

With a hammer and building piece selected, close the piece menu. F4 opens Curve; Shift+F4 opens Mirror; Ctrl+F4 opens Repeat. F4 or Escape exits any mode. Shift+click marks points, L submits, U removes your last shape's unbuilt ghosts, and Backspace removes the last marker. Exit with F4 to build normally with E.

Curve: select a beam/pole and mark start, bend, end. Native-length beams follow the curve in 3D with overlapping joints. Requires two endpoint snap points and a span of 0.25–8 metres.

Mirror: mark two points for a vertical mirror plane, then Ctrl+click built pieces or visible ghosts to toggle source selection. Thin wire boxes show sources; heavier boxes preview copies. Ctrl+Backspace removes the last selection. Reflection axes and pivot compensation follow native snap layouts, fixing triangular under-roof walls and sloped beams while preserving ordinary roof pitch; mesh bounds are a fallback. Asymmetric carvings and handed meshes keep their original geometry, so inspect the ghosts before building.

Repeat: mark start, bend, end with any normal building piece selected. An options panel opens automatically beside the preview: choose a Path anchor (piece origin, visual center, or a native snap point), spacing, yaw, pitch, roll, and whether to turn with the curve. Changes preview live; Confirm submits, Edit path replaces the end, and Cancel exits. Escape closes options without losing the path; L reopens. Slider clicks never mark the world or attack. Spacing fits evenly to both endpoints; the chosen anchor stays on the path after rotation, marked by gold crosses. Choose Bottom for a post base or an end snap for a beam. There is no automatic terrain alignment. Curve-following preserves the seed's initial heading/tilt, then applies rotation offsets. Outside the panel, Ctrl+click copies a built piece/ghost and its orientation; [ / ], Page Up/Down, and Home remain available.

Preview strokes/boxes are local and approximate; L creates full shared planner ghosts. Curves are limited to 128 metres and all tools to 256 pieces. Markers/sources require 40-metre reach; the planner checks all output positions, recipes, wards, and no-build areas. Scaled and terrain-operation pieces are excluded. Selections are pose snapshots.

Terrain and built pieces stay intact; normal materials/support still apply. Undo is one submitted shape across all tools and clears on death, world change, or F6. Only the designer needs BuildShapes; participating players should use updated BuildOrders and the selected pieces. Ghost selection, mirror mesh alignment, multiplayer, and fresh-launch behavior need gameplay testing.
