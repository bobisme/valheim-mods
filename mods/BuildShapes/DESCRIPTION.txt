Draw curves, arches, arcs and straight runs of beams, mirror pieces and repeat decorations as shared BuildOrders ghosts. Requires BuildOrders with its planning and ghost-selection APIs. Hallwright (timber halls, longhouses, stave temples) is its own mod since BuildShapes 0.9.0: install Hallwright for the stake.

Pick a piece in the hammer as usual, then hold Left Ctrl (about a third of a second, without clicking or scrolling) for the shape radial: flick towards Curve, Arch, Arc, Line, Mirror or Repeat and let go (or click). It looks like the game's own radial; it shows only the shapes that work with the piece in your hand, and Build leaves the shape tool. Escape, or letting go in the middle, closes it.

In a shape tool: left click places markers; once they're all down, the shape shows as a sketch (below). Scroll turns them (in the game's 22.5° steps), E plans them as shared ghosts (and uses nothing you're looking at), Delete clears the shape and keeps the tool, Backspace removes the last marker, U removes your last shape's unbuilt ghosts, and Escape leaves the tool. Ctrl+scroll sets Arch's and Arc's height and Repeat's spacing (Shift with it for fine steps). Ctrl+click selects pieces in Mirror and copies one in Repeat; a slow Ctrl+click or Ctrl+scroll that opens the radial still does what it would have done.

Curve: pick any piece with a length (a beam, pole, stone block, wall or pillar) and click start, bend, end. Native-length beams follow the curve in 3D with overlapping joints. Pieces are laid end to end along their own length: the axis their snap points spread furthest along (a stone block lies along the path; a pillar along its height), or their mesh's longest side; 0.25–8 metres long.

Arch: pick a piece with a length and click both ends. The arch rises straight up from them: an upright leg one beam long at each end (three quarters of the height on a low arch), so the end beams stand plumb, and a half-ellipse between the legs' tops, a semicircle when it's half as high as it's wide. Ctrl+scroll sets the height above the ends' middle (0–32 m); a gold guide marks the top. Unequal end heights work; the ends must be apart horizontally.

Arc: like Arch, but the circle through both ends and the top: its ends lean as the circle has them (a half-circle stands up at its ends).

Line: pick a piece with a length and click start and end. Native-length beams run straight between the markers at any slope, or upright; the outer beams meet both markers and the overlaps are spread evenly.

Mirror: click two points for a vertical mirror plane, then Ctrl+click built pieces or visible ghosts to toggle source selection. Thin wire boxes show sources; the mirrored copies show as real pieces. Ctrl+Backspace removes the last selection. Reflection axes and pivot compensation follow native snap layouts, fixing triangular under-roof walls and sloped beams while preserving ordinary roof pitch; mesh bounds are a fallback. Asymmetric carvings and handed meshes keep their original geometry, so inspect the ghosts before building.

Repeat: click start, bend, end with any normal building piece selected. Scroll turns each piece, Ctrl+scroll sets the spacing, Home switches turning with the curve, and Ctrl+click copies a built piece or ghost and its orientation. O opens the full options: path anchor (piece origin, visual center, or a native snap point), spacing, yaw, pitch and roll, with exact values (type, then Enter or Apply, or use the ± buttons; Shift for finer steps). E plans from there too; O or Escape closes it. Spacing fits evenly to both endpoints; the chosen anchor stays on the path after rotation, marked by gold crosses.

Live draft sharing: other players with BuildShapes see your markers and shape previews as cyan visuals labeled with your name. Sharing is on by default; F7 exposes send/receive and piece-preview toggles under Shared drafts. These visuals have no colliders, costs or build actions; E still creates normal shared BuildOrders ghosts. Drafts clear on submission/exit/unload and expire after disconnects. Viewers draw the nearest four drafts within 120 m, with at most 4,096 shared pieces visible.

Sketch preview (the default): the shape is drawn like an architect's watercolour, as Hallwright drafts are: pen strokes along every edge where a piece folds or ends, over a wash of the piece's own colour on watercolour-paper grain. It draws itself in along the shape, ink first, when it's first complete; turning or reshaping it afterwards shows at once. PreviewStyle (F7, General) = Pieces shows the real pieces instead. Either way previews are local only (no colliders, no cost). While a shape tool is out, its keys stand in for the game's key hints along the bottom of the screen, in the same look, with a line above for the shape, its markers and its settings. Curves are limited to 128 metres and every shape to 256 pieces. Markers/sources require 40-metre reach; the planner checks all output positions, recipes, wards, and no-build areas. Scaled and terrain-operation pieces are excluded. Selections are pose snapshots.

Shapes never change terrain; built pieces stay intact and normal materials/support still apply. Undo is one submitted shape across all tools and clears on death, world change, or F6. Only the designer needs BuildShapes; participating players should use updated BuildOrders and the selected pieces. Keys are in F7 (Controls): ShapeMenu (Left Ctrl), ConfirmShape (E), RepeatOptions (O). ClaudeTools: shapes radial [wedge|off] holds the radial open, and shapes hints <tool|off> [full] shows a tool's key hints, for pictures.
