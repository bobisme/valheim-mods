Draw curves with ordinary beams and poles, then build the shared ghosts through BuildOrders.

Requires BuildOrders with add-on support. Select a beam or pole in your hammer, close the piece menu, and press F4 to enter Curve mode. Left Shift + left click marks the start, bend, then end, on the ground or existing building pieces. Nearby building snap points are used automatically. L submits the curve as ordinary BuildOrders ghosts; build them with E and normal materials. Backspace changes the last marker. F4 or Escape exits Curve mode.

U in Curve mode removes the unbuilt ghosts from your last submitted curve; already built pieces stay. Undo is one step for the current session and clears on reload, death/respawn, or world changes. You can also remove ghosts through BuildOrders' Plans window.

The turquoise preview shows beam center lines. Pieces keep their original lengths, with overlaps at joints, and the first and last endpoints meet your markers. The bend marker lies on the smooth underlying curve. Works in three dimensions, including vertical arches. Shorter beams follow tight bends more closely. Supports beams/poles with exactly two endpoint snap points, up to 8 metres long; curves are limited to 128 metres and 256 pieces. Tight or degenerate curves are rejected before submission.

Nothing is built automatically and terrain is unchanged. Existing piece recipes, ward permissions, no-build areas, and materials still apply. Only the designer needs BuildShapes; participating players should use BuildOrders with the new add-on API and the selected building pieces. Older planners can build the ghosts but still expose blueprint terrain actions. Public planning-interface and multiplayer behavior still need in-game playtesting.
