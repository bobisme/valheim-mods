Draw curves and arches, mirror pieces, repeat decorations, and design a whole Viking timber hall from a floor plan as shared BuildOrders ghosts. Requires BuildOrders with its planning and ghost-selection APIs.

With a hammer and building piece selected, close the piece menu. F4 opens a picker for Hallwright, Curve, Arch, Mirror, and Repeat. Choose a mode; modifiers are no longer needed. F4/Escape closes the picker, and selecting the current mode resumes its preview. Switching modes clears the local preview; submitted ghosts remain. Use Exit shape mode in the picker, or Escape outside options, to return to normal building. Shift+click marks points, L submits, U removes your last shape's unbuilt ghosts, and Backspace removes the last marker. Exit shape mode to build normally with E.

Hallwright: choose it in F4 with the hammer equipped, then Shift+click square corners in boundary order. The first edge sets the rotated 2-metre grid; subsequent edges snap to its two directions. Concave L/U outlines work. L opens the settings, or click the first corner again to finish drawing. Backspace removes a corner; Delete clears the outline. Adjust walls (2–4 m), roof pitch (26°/45°), Gabled/Tiered silhouette, entrance type and edge, foundation lift and an intricacy dial (Simple, Crafted, Ornate, Grand). Exact foundation lift and fine nudges use the same numeric controls as Arch/Repeat. The shell previews using the real meshes, with materials/ghost and roof visibility buttons. Auto entrances use an unlocked 3 m gate when wall height is 3 m or taller; choose Door or Gate explicitly. Gates require at least 3 m walls. The opening and header fit the actual native piece height. Tiered roofs raise the central roof by 1 m above low side aisles on wings at least 6 m wide; matching gables, vertical roof-step walls, shaped rafters and structural inner colonnades support the raised tier. Narrow wings stay gabled. Its floors, exterior walls and entrance, joined rectangular roof wings, gable infill, trusses, terrain-reaching foundations and entrance steps are fitted together. Small odd-width gable peaks are intentionally vented. Intricacy adds knee braces, radiating gable timberwork, darkwood arches and raven crests when unlocked.

Materials default to Auto, based on your character's known hammer recipes. Stronger core-wood posts, stone plinths, darkwood details and reinforced ridge supports are used only when unlocked. Select Timber/Core wood/Stone base/Darkwood to restrict the style. A live normal-cost materials bill and station list accompany the preview. Unlock changes refresh automatically. Foundations raise the floor above the highest sampled ground; terrain and existing pieces stay intact. Native collider contacts, actual material support losses and nearby built supports estimate whether the complete shell stands; extra interior ridge columns are added when necessary, and unresolved collapsing pieces block acceptance. Confirm rechecks the current terrain, support and unlocks, then creates one shared ghost plan and returns to normal building. This estimate is not a substitute for the game's final placement checks.

Hallwright supports 24 corners, 64 floor tiles (256 m²), 12 roof wings and 1,024 pieces including foundations, within 70 m of the designer. Diagonal/self-crossing outlines, unloaded or protected ground, excessive foundations and stretched beams are rejected. Large shells need BuildOrders' TryCreateBuildingShell API (provided in the Hallwright planner PR); older planners can accept shells up to their 256-piece cap. U removes the last plan's unbuilt ghosts; select Hallwright again after confirming to use U. Only local preview state is cleared on F6, death, world changes or switching modes. ClaudeTools exposes hall catalog/rectangle/outline/options/status/ui/clear/confirm/undo and a hallpreview camera frame for testing.

Curve: select a beam/pole and mark start, bend, end. Native-length beams follow the curve in 3D with overlapping joints. Requires two endpoint snap points and a span of 0.25–8 metres.

Arch: select a beam/pole and Shift+click start and end. The height panel opens automatically. Center rise (0–32 m) is measured above the line between the endpoints; a gold guide marks the midpoint. Adjust the slider, type an exact height then Enter/Apply, or use ±0.05 m buttons (Shift: 0.01 m). The preview updates live, keeping both endpoints fixed. Confirm applies pending edits and creates shared ghosts. Edit end keeps your chosen rise; Escape closes the panel, L reopens it, and Cancel exits; F4 opens the mode picker. This is a vertical parabolic curve; unequal endpoint heights are supported. Beams keep their native lengths with overlapping joints, so the center line approximates the curve. Ends must be horizontally separated, and tight bends or too-long beams are rejected.

Mirror: mark two points for a vertical mirror plane, then Ctrl+click built pieces or visible ghosts to toggle source selection. Thin wire boxes show sources; heavier boxes preview copies. Ctrl+Backspace removes the last selection. Reflection axes and pivot compensation follow native snap layouts, fixing triangular under-roof walls and sloped beams while preserving ordinary roof pitch; mesh bounds are a fallback. Asymmetric carvings and handed meshes keep their original geometry, so inspect the ghosts before building.

Repeat: mark start, bend, end with any normal building piece selected. An options panel opens automatically beside the preview: choose a Path anchor (piece origin, visual center, or a native snap point), spacing, yaw, pitch, roll, and whether to turn with the curve. Changes preview live. For precise adjustments, type a value then Enter/Apply, or use ± buttons (0.05 m / 1°; Shift: 0.01 m / 0.1°). Sliders keep exact values until dragged. Confirm applies pending numeric entries and submits, Edit path replaces the end, and Cancel exits. F4 opens the mode picker. Escape closes options without losing the path; L reopens. Slider clicks never mark the world or attack. Spacing fits evenly to both endpoints; the chosen anchor stays on the path after rotation, marked by gold crosses. Choose Bottom for a post base or an end snap for a beam. There is no automatic terrain alignment. Curve-following preserves the seed's initial heading/tilt, then applies rotation offsets. Outside the panel, Ctrl+click copies a built piece/ghost and its orientation; [ / ], Page Up/Down, and Home remain available.

Preview strokes/boxes are local and approximate; L creates full shared planner ghosts. Curves are limited to 128 metres and Curve/Arch/Mirror/Repeat to 256 pieces; Hallwright has the larger bounds above. Markers/sources require 40-metre reach; the planner checks all output positions, recipes, wards, and no-build areas. Scaled and terrain-operation pieces are excluded. Selections are pose snapshots.

Terrain and built pieces stay intact; normal materials/support still apply. Undo is one submitted shape across all tools and clears on death, world change, or F6. Only the designer needs BuildShapes; participating players should use updated BuildOrders and the selected pieces. Ghost selection, mirror mesh alignment, multiplayer, and fresh-launch behavior need gameplay testing.
