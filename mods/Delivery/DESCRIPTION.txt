Delivery: hot food, brought to you. Make an Errand Horn at a workbench (4 wood, 2 leather scraps, 1 resin) and use it (hotbar or inventory). Its menu lists every cooked meal you know: what it fills, its price in coins, and its call, drawn as notes. Every meal has its own call on the horn, and that call is how the courier knows what you want. Blow the call for each portion (up to ten; you raise the horn to your lips for each), then the end call: "That's all!"

A horn answers from far off: your courier has heard you. After a while (a minute when he thinks middling well of you) he sets out, and you'll see him running in from the distance. He goes round trees and houses, swims where he must, and comes straight through when he's stuck. He hands over the food (on the ground at your feet if your bags are full) and takes the coins: about a coin for every six points of health, stamina and eitr a meal fills, plus a fee for the run. The fee is 5 coins in the Meadows and more where the way is dangerous (up to 45 in the Ashlands and the Deep North), half again at night. You pay when he arrives; if you can't, he takes the food back and thinks less of you.

Then he waits for a tip. Tip him and he comes quicker and runs faster next time; don't, and he takes his time. He goes from Sullen to Grudging, Steady, Eager and Devoted, and a devoted courier sometimes throws in an extra portion. He's your courier: same name, same face, every time, in a bright orange tunic so you see him coming, and on your map while he's out.

Only meals you know how to make (or have had in hand) can be ordered; F7 → Mod settings can open the whole menu. He won't come down into dungeons, and he waits outside while you're in one. Other players near you hear your calls (each player needs the mod to hear them); only you see your courier. He isn't a creature: he can't be hurt and doesn't fight, and nothing of his is saved. Settings: how long he takes, how far off he starts, how long he waits for a tip, the horn's volume, and how far others hear it.
