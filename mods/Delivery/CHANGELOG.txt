0.1.5: The horn's notes swell in slowly (breath building, not a tongued attack), with a gentler scoop up into pitch.
0.1.4: The horn sounds raspier and darker: breath comes through the tone, a little rasp in it, and the high end rolled off.
0.1.3: The end call ("That's all!") can be pressed as soon as there's an order. It used to stay greyed out after the first call, because it waited for the calls to finish sounding and the menu never looked again. The end call still sounds after the others.
0.1.2: The Errand Horn looks like a horn in menus and on the ground: drawn side-on and slanting across its slot (it was seen end-on, a stub), dark at the tip shading to pale at the bell, thicker, with a thicker strap.
0.1.1: The horn's menu no longer draws each meal's call as notes: just the meal, what it fills, its price and Blow.
0.1.0: First Delivery. An Errand Horn (workbench: 4 wood, 2 leather scraps, 1 resin) orders cooked meals: use it, blow each meal's own call (its notes drawn beside it, the horn raised to your lips), then the end call. A courier answers from the distance, sets out after a while, runs in wearing a bright orange tunic and shown on your map (round trees and houses, swimming where he must) and hands over the food for coins: about a coin for every six points it fills, plus a run fee by biome, half again at night. Tip him and he comes quicker next time; don't, and he takes his time. A devoted courier sometimes throws in an extra. Other players hear your calls.
