Somebody's sci-fi kit fell through a crack into the tenth world. None of it belongs here, and all of it works. Made at the forge (the speeder with the hammer); every number and cost is a setting.

Laser Pistol: one-handed, red bolts, one per click, overheats if you lean on it. Laser Rifle: two-handed; hold attack to charge, release to fire a blue beam out to 260 m; a full charge burns through four bodies; hold block to shoulder it and zoom. Both guns are held in both hands, aim where you look, kick, and vent when overheated.
Laser Sword: the game's sword swings with a glowing blade that hums louder the faster it moves, ignites when drawn, chops trees and breaks rock. Blue, green, red, violet, yellow, white or cyan.
Jetpack (utility slot): jump, then hold Jump to fly; move keys steer, Run boosts. Fuel refills on the ground. No fall damage while you wear it. Everyone sees the flames.
Infrared Goggles (helmet): thermal view. The world goes dim and blue, and every warm body within 80 m glows through walls and hills; you see at night. H switches them.
Combat Drone: use it to launch a drone that circles your shoulder and zaps enemies that mean harm; use it again to call it home. Its battery recharges in your bag. One drone per item.
Orbital Uplink: point at the ground up to 300 m away and paint it. Three seconds later a low orbit ion cannon puts a pillar of light there: 600 damage over 12 m, fells trees, breaks rocks, sets things burning and digs a crater. 60 s to recharge. Spares buildings, and digs no crater near a ward or your builds unless you allow it.
Hover Speeder (hammer, near a forge): rides 1 m over land and water. W/S throttle, A/D steer, Shift boost, Jump hops; a friend can ride pillion. Rams enemies at speed.
Gravity Gun: hold attack to lift a creature, log, cart or loose item and carry it; block flings it. Flung things hurt what they hit.
Displacer: blink to the crosshair, up to 60 m, every 3 s.
Stim Pack: full health and stamina now, then 30 s of six times the regeneration and 40% more speed.
Recall Beacon: one use, takes you to your bed, ore and all.

Friends are never hurt (tamed animals; other players only with PvP on both sides). Everything is seen and heard by other players. Native Linux and Windows.
