0.2.2: The infrared goggles sit on the eyes and move with the face (they hung in front of it, and didn't nod or turn with the head): they now hang from the helmet joint as the game's own helmets do.

0.2.1: The laser sword sits right in the hand: the hilt runs through the fist with the blade pointing forward (it stuck out sideways).

0.2.0: Every gadget has a new detailed model, made in Blender: the pistol (finned barrel with glowing coils, power cell), rifle (scope, skeleton stock, coil rings), laser sword hilt, jetpack (welded tanks, valves, gauge, harness, bell nozzles), night-vision goggles, combat drone (spinning props, ball-turret eye), orbital uplink (dish, screen, antenna), hover speeder (faired body, windscreen, nacelles, repulsor pads), gravity gun, displacer, stim pack and recall beacon. The ion cannon strike looks the part: a soft tapered pillar with spiralling rings, a blooming flash, molten ground rings, sparks and steam. Lights, screens and coils glow properly. The pistol and uplink sit right in the hand, the goggles over the eyes, and in thermal view you see yourself only faintly. Losing the jetpack in mid-air no longer means a fatal fall: fall protection lasts until you land. The rifle beam starts at its muzzle. New test commands: wu look, bones, grip, mat.

0.1.1: Items show their own icons (the pictures were drawn but thrown away as empty: the game's material leaves the background's alpha alone, so it is now worked out from a picture on black and one on white, as Quad's Guns does). Hot reload no longer turns worn and held gadgets and placed speeders magenta. New test command: wu icons.

0.1.0: First version. Laser pistol, laser rifle, laser sword, jetpack, infrared goggles, combat drones, orbital uplink (low orbit ion cannon), hover speeder, gravity gun, displacer, stim pack and recall beacon. Not yet played in the game: expect rough edges in how things sit in the hand and on the body, the tuning, and the look of the glows.
