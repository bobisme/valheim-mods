Shipyard (Naust): build boats at the shore, in stages you can see.

Slipway: building stocks in the hammer's Misc tab (workbench; 20 wood, 6 stone). Build it with its bow end towards the water. Press E to lay down a keel, then E again to put in what the stage under way needs from your bag. Any player can put in, as much as they have; a stage is only built once it's paid in full, and what's put in towards it is kept on the slipway. Each finished stage stands on the stocks: keel and stems, then ribs, then the strakes, then the thwarts and oars.

Launching: when she's finished, E launches her into the water ahead of the slipway's bow end, wherever it's first deep enough within 24 m. With no water there, she waits on the stocks.

Færing: a little four-oared rowing boat, quick and light, for two (one at the steering oar, one on the fore thwart). Rowed, never sailed: forward stops at rowing pace, which is faster than a karve's. 50 wood, 10 resin, 8 leather scraps in all, over four stages. Broken up, she gives her makings back, as the game's ships do.

Taking the slipway down gives back everything put into the boat on it, finished stages and all. In a world where building costs nothing, E builds each stage for nothing, and taking the slipway down gives nothing back, as with the hammer.

Coming: the knarr (a wide cargo ship), a boathouse that keeps moored ships from wearing, repairs at the slipway, and Jarldom's shipwright building on it.

Removing Shipyard: as with any mod that adds things, its slipways and boats are gone once a place is loaded without it (what was put into a slipway with them).
