Shipyard (Naust): build boats at the shore, in stages you can see.

Slipway: building stocks in the hammer's Misc tab (workbench; 20 wood, 6 stone). Build it with its bow end towards the water. Shift+E on an empty slipway picks the hull (færing or knarr); press E to lay down a keel, then E again to put in what the stage under way needs from your bag. Any player can put in, as much as they have; a stage is only built once it's paid in full, and what's put in towards it is kept on the slipway. Each finished stage stands on the stocks: keel and stems, then ribs, then the strakes, then the fittings.

Launching: when she's finished, E launches her into the water ahead of the slipway's bow end, wherever it's first deep enough, all along her length, and clear of other boats (18 m past the slipway, plus her own length). With no water there, she waits on the stocks.

Færing: a little four-oared rowing boat, quick and light, for two (one at the steering oar, one on the fore thwart). Rowed, never sailed: forward stops at rowing pace, which is faster than a karve's. 50 wood, 10 resin, 8 leather scraps in all, over four stages. Broken up, she gives her makings back, as the game's ships do.

Taking the slipway down gives back everything put into the boat on it, finished stages and all. In a world where building costs nothing, E builds each stage for nothing, and taking the slipway down gives nothing back, as with the hammer.

Knarr: a broad, deep cargo ship with one big square sail, bronze-nailed (so after the karve). Her hold takes 8x6 (the longship's 6x3), a ladder over each side amidships down into it, four seats on the sea chests. Slow in light airs, strong in a gale: her sail draws 0.6 of a longship's in a calm, up to 1.25 in strong wind. The sail is set and furled with the speed (half, full). 40 fine wood, 22 resin, 120 bronze nails, 30 wood, 15 deer hide, 10 leather scraps over five stages (keel and stems, ribs and beams, strakes, decks and hold, mast and sail).

Naust: a boathouse (hammer, Misc; workbench; 24 round logs, 40 wood, 8 resin), open at its water end. A ship moored inside it isn't worn by the shore and the waves.

Repairs: the hammer mends a ship within 30 m of a slipway, as it does near a workbench.

Jarldom: with Jarldom, a colony's shipwright builds slipway hulls at the boatyard, fetching each stage's makings from the stores, and launches them. Other mods can do the same through Shipyard.Api.

With Homespun: the knarr's mast stage takes one of its wool sails in place of the deer hide, and she sails under its colour (the stripes in the dye's colour, brown for undyed wool), on the stocks and at sea.

Removing Shipyard: as with any mod that adds things, its slipways and boats are gone once a place is loaded without it (what was put into a slipway with them).
