0.1.0: first release. A slipway (hammer, Misc: 20 wood, 6 stone) where boats are built at the shore in stages you can see, and paid for by anyone, from their bag: the færing first, a four-oared rowing boat for two in four stages (keel and stems, ribs, strakes, thwarts and oars; 50 wood, 10 resin, 8 leather scraps in all). Finished, E launches her ahead of the slipway's bow end, where the water is deep enough all along her length. Rowed, never sailed. Taken down, the slipway gives back all that was put in; where building costs nothing, stages cost nothing and nothing comes back. Its own models for the slipway and each stage. Live-checked: laying, paying and launching by E and by the test commands, all four stages on the stocks, the færing floating level at her waterline in waves, rowing (about 2.7 m/s, stops at rowing pace; back and stop) with a player at the helm, the take-down refund (22 wood, 2 resin for two stages; checked before the free-build rule, so in Creative it now gives nothing back). Not yet tried: building with real materials by hand, sitting on the fore thwart, the ladder, steering, a second player putting in.
