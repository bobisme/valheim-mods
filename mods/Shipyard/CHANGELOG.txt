0.3.3: Api.StageCosts gives the wool sail in place of the hides when Homespun is in, as the slipway asks for it (Jarldom's ship costs said 15 deer hide). Live-checked: Jarldom's knarr cost reads 1 wool sail.

0.3.2: wool sails, with Homespun 0.3.0. The knarr's mast stage takes a Homespun wool sail in place of the deer hide (any colour; taken down, the same colour comes back), and she sails under its colour: the stripes in the dye's colour, brown for undyed wool, on the stocks and once launched. Test commands: yard pay [stages] [colour], yard clear. Live-checked: a knarr paid with a blue sail shows blue and white stripes furled on the stocks. Not yet tried: a launched knarr's sail colour at sea (the boatyard's water was taken), paying the sail by hand.

0.3.1: test tools only: yard row takes the helm of whatever boat the player stands on (to compare with the game's ships) and reports her speed and the wind. Measured with it, wind dead astern at Full: the knarr makes about 3.7 m/s in light wind (0.2) to the longship's 5.9, and 8.2 to its 9.1 in a gale (0.9); her sail sets half and full and furls at stop.

0.3.0: the knarr, the naust, repairs, and an Api (0.2.0 and 0.3.0 together). Knarr: a broad cargo ship with one big square sail, five stages on the slipway (Shift+E on an empty slipway picks the hull), hold 8x6, a ladder each side, four seats; her sail draws less than a longship's in light airs and more in a gale, and is set and furled with the speed. Naust: a boathouse (hammer, Misc) that keeps a moored ship from wear by the shore and the waves. The hammer mends a ship within 30 m of a slipway. Shipyard.Api lets other mods lay keels, put in stages and launch (Jarldom's shipwright uses it). Launching needs water clear of other boats, and the reach grows with the hull. Fixes: the port strakes of both hulls were bare sheets, invisible from inside; both hulls now keep the sea out of their insides (a water mask). Live-checked: every knarr stage on the stocks, launch, the knarr riding at her waterline with a dry hold, the hold's size, the naust sheltering a færing (no harm from a bump inside it, 2% outside), mending near a slipway, a Jarldom shipwright fetching, building all four færing stages and launching her. Not yet tried by a player: sailing the knarr (speeds, the sail's set), her ladders and seats, naming her at the helm.

0.1.0: first release. A slipway (hammer, Misc: 20 wood, 6 stone) where boats are built at the shore in stages you can see, and paid for by anyone, from their bag: the færing first, a four-oared rowing boat for two in four stages (keel and stems, ribs, strakes, thwarts and oars; 50 wood, 10 resin, 8 leather scraps in all). Finished, E launches her ahead of the slipway's bow end, where the water is deep enough all along her length. Rowed, never sailed. Taken down, the slipway gives back all that was put in; where building costs nothing, stages cost nothing and nothing comes back. Its own models for the slipway and each stage. Live-checked: laying, paying and launching by E and by the test commands, all four stages on the stocks, the færing floating level at her waterline in waves, rowing (about 2.7 m/s, stops at rowing pace; back and stop) with a player at the helm, the take-down refund (22 wood, 2 resin for two stages; checked before the free-build rule, so in Creative it now gives nothing back). Not yet tried: building with real materials by hand, sitting on the fore thwart, the ladder, steering, a second player putting in.
