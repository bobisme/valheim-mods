Shipyard naust: a 22 m timber boat shed at the shore. Its land end stands on the ground and is closed by a plank wall. Its sea end
is open over the water, so a ship floats in and out there. A 1.0 m slot runs open along the whole ridge, so a standing mast (up to
12 m) passes through the roof.
Made by naust.py in Blender, baked low-poly by tools/bake (docs/model-baking.md). naust.py copies the helpers and export from
yard.py. It is kept apart from yard.py/bake_conf.py/README.txt and has its own work directory, ~/.cache/shipyard_naust.

  Export (high, untextured; add "render" for previews):
    mkdir -p ~/.cache/shipyard_naust && cp mods/Shipyard/Models/naust.py ~/.cache/shipyard_naust/
    blender -b --python ~/.cache/shipyard_naust/naust.py -- [render] naust      (writes ~/.cache/shipyard_naust/naust.json.gz)
    previews: ~/.cache/shipyard_naust/naust_{sea34,side,inside,land34,bare34}.png ("bare34" has no water or shore)
  Bake (what ships: naust.json.gz, naust_albedo.png, naust_normal.png here):
    blender -b --python $PWD/tools/bake/bake.py -- $PWD/mods/Shipyard/Models/naust_bake_conf.py naust
    (Run it from the main checkout: tools/bake/tex is git-ignored and a worktree doesn't have it. The bake copies naust.py itself.)

Materials (slots, as yard.py): 0 pine (wall and gable boards), 1 oak (posts, plates, tie beams, short posts, rafters, ridge timbers,
braces, rail, bollards), 2 tar (the roof planks), 3 iron (bolt heads: decals, baked into the texture only).
The look is grey, weathered timber. naust_bake_conf.py tints the game's woodpole picture (which is brown) with tints over 1 in
green and blue to cancel the brown: pine light grey, oak darker grey, tarred planks near black.

Triangles: high 8,060 -> baked 3,140 (BUDGET 2600 asked: with less than that, every bevelled timber collapses to a plain 12-triangle
box; at 3500 they stayed at 20 and the bake came to 4,028). Texture 512 px. At the slipway's 256 px, this 22 m shed's ~1,450 UV
islands came out 1-2 texels thick, and whole wall boards baked as black stripes. 512 is the faering strakes' size, and it bakes clean.

Frame (game: +x right, +y up, +z toward the sea / the open end). Origin on the centreline at ground/water level, mid-length.
