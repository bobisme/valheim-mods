0.11.0: The sketch draws itself in: new pieces appear bottom up, ink first, then their colour washes in from the bare paper. The whole hall when the outline becomes valid or the draft opens; only the added pieces when a setting adds some. Faint construction lines carry the floor, wall tops, eaves and ridge a little past the corners and gables. Roofs light as clean slopes instead of crumpled paper. Over a sketch, the corner, door and chimney markers and the outline's dashes are red chalk and the live line is ink, instead of glowing amber and white. hall status reports the drawing-in (sketch).

0.10.0: Sketch preview, now the default: the draft is drawn like a Piranesi watercolour. Pen strokes ink every edge where a piece folds or ends (running past the corners, bending a little, sometimes gone over twice) over a wash of the piece's own colour softened towards the paper, on watercolour-paper grain. It's solid and lit like the world around it; pieces that would fall are inked red on pink. Materials, Ghosts and Layout are still in the Preview row. Saved drafts open in Sketch.

0.9.0: Hallwright on its own, split out of BuildShapes (it was BuildShapes 0.8.10's Hallwright). Pick the Hallwright stake in the hammer's build menu (Misc): click corners, Shift+click entrances, Ctrl+click smoke holes or chimneys, E inside the outline (or L) for the settings, where the style row picks Timber hall, Longhouse or Stave temple. There's no F4 entry any more. Saved drafts and ground records are the same files, and the first start takes your Hallwright, key and shared-draft settings from BuildShapes' config. Update BuildShapes to 0.9.0 too: older BuildShapes still has its own stake. Live hall drafts are shared between Hallwright players.
