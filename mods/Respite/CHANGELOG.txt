0.1.0: First Respite. One raid clock for the world: after a raid, no other comes within 500 m for 1.5 in-game days, and at most three in a week near one place, counting the game's raids and those of Omens, Jarldom and Shieldwall (the game's own timer skips resting bases; the mods wait). Raiders gather outside the base for 90 seconds first, told where and pinned on the map, then come from there; the game's raids are held for the warning and last that much longer. Respite.Api (version 1) for other mods: Blocked, Raided, Muster, MusterFrom, WarningSeconds.
