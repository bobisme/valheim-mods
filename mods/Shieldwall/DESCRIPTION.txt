A game within the game: build a Warstone, plant magic staves around it, and hold the line when a horde comes for it.

The Warstone (hammer, misc; 30 stone, 10 wood, 5 flint, 5 resin, near a workbench) is a standing stone that wards your home. Everyone within 40 metres gets faster health and stamina regeneration, and as the stone grows, carry weight, eitr regeneration and armor. Set it under a roof with a fire burning within 10 metres and the ward is twice as strong and adds comfort. One Warstone to a home.

Shift+E on the stone sounds its war horn. A rift opens 60 to 95 metres away and a horde gathers, the strongest your world has unlocked: greydwarfs at first, then the Black Forest's host, the drowned dead, a mountain pack, a Fuling warband, the swarm of the mist and the Charred legion. After 90 seconds it marches on the stone in four to eight waves, ending with a warchief. A glowing line shows the road it will take and bends as you build: walls turn it aside, and where the road is shut a red line marks where they will break through. They fight what stands close, smash what blocks the way, and strike the stone when they reach it. Sappers go for your staves; bats, drakes, deathsquitos and gjall fly over the walls. A bar at the top of the screen counts the waves and the stone's strength, with the game's own raid music.

Hold until the last of them falls and spoils appear at the stone's foot: a chest of that land with warshards, coins and materials. Each siege held adds a mark: the ward grows, the horde grows, and new staves unlock. If they break the stone it cracks: its ward weakens until it holds a siege again. Nothing is lost.

Staves are crafted at the Warstone with warshards (raiders carry them; warchiefs always do), so the first siege is fought by hand. Hold one and press Use on open ground to plant it within 50 metres of a stone; Use it again to pull it out. Planted staves fire on raiders and monsters in reach and never hurt players, companions, tames or buildings:
Ember stave (no marks): fireballs that splash.
Frost stave (1 mark): a fast stream of ice shards that chill.
Hearth stave (1 mark): mends you, your companions, your tames and, in a siege, the stone.
Thunder stave (2 marks): heavy bolts at the strongest foe in 42 metres.
Blast stave (3 marks): bombs that burst over the crowd.
Upgrade them at the stone for more power and reach. Any other staff that throws something can be planted too, weakly.

Once you have a Warstone, a base raid that would hit near it becomes a siege at the stone instead, with three minutes' warning (needs Shieldwall on the host).

Sieges run on the game of the player nearest the stone, so they work on a dedicated server without the mod; everyone who joins in needs Shieldwall. Warnings, wave pacing, the most raiders at once, the time limit and the horn's cooldown are in F7.
