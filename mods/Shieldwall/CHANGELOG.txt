0.2.0: Towers. War staves are now huge relics too heavy to wield, set in stave sockets: stone pedestals built on the ground, walls or tower tops, 5 m apart. Use a socket to set or take out a stave; Shift+Use strengthens it in place with warshards. The Warstone has a level (1-5) raised by four upgrade pieces built beside it (war banner, trophy totem, horned brazier, rune obelisk; the horde barely scratches them); it feeds 2-10 staves within 26-50 m, nearest first, and the level gates stave recipes and strengthening. Sappers go for sockets and tear down the tower under one that stands high; a raider a stave keeps hitting may turn on its tower; warchiefs strike buildings half again as hard. Staves aim at whoever is closest to the stone (Thunder at the strongest) and count their kills. Shift+E on the stone calls the next wave early for bonus warshards, and its hover names what the next wave holds. The siege bar is now the game's own boss bar. Staves planted in the ground by 0.1 fall over as items, ready for a socket.

0.1.2: Quicker sieges: waves come at most 40 s apart (was 75; existing settings keep their value), and one or two raiders stuck out of sight end a siege after 75 s instead of 150. The spoils chest appears 4.5 m from the stone on the side away from the rift, out of the next horde's swing. A clearer warshard icon. More Claude Tools checks: sound the horn, plant what you hold, open the stone's menu, and fire a base raid at the stone to see it turn into a siege. Live-tested: the horn, raids drawn to the stone, recipe discovery from a warshard, 60 fps through a siege.

0.1.1: A held siege now ends with a summary (how many fell, how the stone stood, its new rank, how many warshards it left) and a burst of light at the stone. The Hearth stave mends a besieged stone by half a percent of its strength each pulse (more when upgraded) instead of a token 30. Test helpers for Claude Tools: start a chosen horde, place a stone at coordinates, remove one.

0.1.0: First release. The Warstone (ward, crafting station, war horn), sieges in waves from a rift with a shown road and breach point, seven hordes by world progress with sappers, flyers and warchiefs, spoils and marks, cracking, five plantable staves with upgrades, raids drawn to the stone, a siege bar and raid music.
