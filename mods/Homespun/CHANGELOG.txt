0.1.1: retted flax is a sheaf of grey stalks tied round the middle, line flax a twisted golden strick with a loose knot. The five garments lie folded in their own looks (the undertunic pale linen, the tunic with its red band, the dress with its brooches and beads, the trousers with their drawstring, the cloak with its hood and ring-pin) instead of the base's brown slab, and their icons are drawn from those. Worn, their pictures are recoloured to bleached linen or undyed wool, the body-painted part too, where 0.1.0's tint did nothing.

0.1.0: the first cut, H1. Live-checked: loads without errors, stations render in the game. Flax: retting pool, flax brake, the spinning wheel or a drop spindle, linen thread. Wool: combed from a lox pelt at the flax brake (or Byre's fleece, with Byre), spun into yarn. The warp-weighted loom weaves linen and wool bolts and woven band; the sewing table makes an undertunic, tunic, dress, trousers and hooded cloak, and Hildir's coloured clothes from cloth. A spun command for Claude Tools. Not yet tried in play: the stations' use, the spindle in hand, garments worn.
