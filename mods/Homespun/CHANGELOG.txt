0.2.0: dyeing. A dye vat (12 wood, 3 stone, 2 resin): open it as a chest, put in bolts or clothes and one dyestuff, and a minute later they come out dyed: blueberries blue, raspberries red, dandelions yellow, lichen purple. A bolt takes a measure (5 berries or dandelions, 3 lichen), a garment the bolts in it; a garment can be dyed again, its wear and mending kept. Dyed bolts sew into garments of their colour at the sewing table. Dyed things are items of their own (28 new: 2 bolts and 5 garments in 4 colours), so the colour shows worn, dropped, in chests and for every player with nothing more to sync. Lichen grows on stones in the Mountains and the Black Forest (newly explored ground) and grows back slowly. Models of its own for the vat (the dye in it shows the dyestuff's colour), lichen and the lichen stone. Api 2: the dyes, what a thing dyed a colour becomes. Live-checked: bolts dyed blue (2 of 3 with 12 berries, as the dye ran to), a tunic dyed blue then purple with lichen and worn by a villager, lichen stones in fresh Mountains and Black Forest, the dyed recipes at the sewing table. Not yet tried: opening the vat and its hover by hand, sewing from dyed bolts, a second player seeing the colours.

0.1.4: nettle, the cloth plant you have from the start. Stinging nettle grows wild in meadow clearings and at the Black Forest's edge (newly explored ground only: the game plants a zone once) and grows back after picking; greydwarfs now and then carry a bundle (1 in 10). Sow a nettle with the cultivator in the Meadows or the Black Forest and it grows in under an hour into a stand that gives 2 or 3. Nettle is retted in the pool and broken at the brake as flax is, into retted and line nettle; line nettle spins into 1 linen thread on the spinning wheel or the drop spindle, half what a line flax gives. Its own models: a stand of nettle and a cut bundle tied with flax; retted and line nettle are flax's looks in a greyer green. Live-checked: the plants and items load, the models render, the pool, brake and wheel take nettle. Not yet tried in play: picking, sowing and growing, wild spawns, the greydwarf drop, a full run through the stations.
