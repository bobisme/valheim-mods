0.1.0: First Spyglass. Craft it at the forge from 3 bronze. With it in your inventory, Shift+Z raises it to your eye: the view narrows to 4× optical magnification (wheel: 2–8×), turning slows to match, and a round eyepiece darkens the screen edges. Every quarter second the point you gaze at, up to 600 metres away, uncovers a 30-metre circle of your map. Escape or Shift+Z lowers it. A plain Z from GearSlots and similar mods does not fire while Shift+Z is pressed.
