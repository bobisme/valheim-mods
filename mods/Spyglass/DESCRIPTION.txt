A bronze spyglass. Craft it at the forge from 3 bronze. While it is in your inventory, Shift+Z raises it to your eye; Shift+Z or Escape lowers it.

Looking through it narrows the view to 4× optical magnification. The mouse wheel changes it from 2× to 8×, mouse turning slows to match, and a round eyepiece darkens the edges of the screen. The view is from your eyes, so your character never blocks it. You can still walk while looking.

Where you gaze, your map fills in a little: every quarter second, a 30-metre circle around the point you look at is uncovered, up to 600 metres away. The gaze stops at loaded buildings, trees and ground, and beyond them at the world's generated terrain or sea level. Looking at the sky uncovers nothing. Uncovered map is saved with your character like normal exploring. Radius, range, magnification and the eyepiece can be changed in F7.

Shift+Z takes priority over a plain Z from other mods' shortcut helpers, such as GearSlots' quick slot 1, only while Shift is held. The spyglass is closed by opening the inventory, map, menu or chat, by dying, or by losing the item.

Only the player using it needs the mod for looking and crafting. A spyglass dropped on the ground is a world object: the host and nearby players need the mod too, or a host without it deletes it when its area loads. Removing the mod removes spyglasses from saved inventories. Compiled and checked against the local game; the model, icon, view, map reveal and GearSlots interaction still need in-game playtests.
