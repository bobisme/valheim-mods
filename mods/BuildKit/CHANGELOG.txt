0.3.0: the shingle roofs, the dragon ridge ends and the stave wall, post and portal are gone: the game has its own (the darkwood roofs, the dragon and raven carvings, the Deep North's stave set). Any of them standing in a world disappear. The wattle and daub wall no longer shows the sky in thin cracks round its clay: the panels now run into the timbers.

0.2.3: a hot reload (for mod makers) no longer leaves the old copy's pieces, meshes, materials and icons behind: a few seconds after the new copy is in the hammer it puts its own looks on the roofs and walls standing and frees the rest.

0.2.2: Wattle fence (withies woven between stakes) and wattle and daub wall (clay over wattle in a timber frame).

0.2.1: Stave church pieces: a stave wall of upright tarred planks between a sill and a wall plate, a round stave post with a carved band and capital, and a carved portal: the game's own door in jambs wound with vines, a beast's head on each, a carved arch above.

0.2.0: Shingle roofs in every shape, 26° and 45°: tarred wooden shingles with rounded tongues, as a stave church is roofed, lapping row over row (no seams between pieces), ridges capped with boards, hips and valleys lined with them. A shingle ridge end with a carved dragon's head reaching out from the gable, jaws open (26° and 45°).

0.1.2: No more build-menu errors from a piece an older copy of the mod left behind in the hammer's list.

0.1.1: Turf ridge smoke vents (26° and 45°): a ridge piece with a slot open its whole length and a single-pitch hood over it, where a house's smoke actually gathers. Put them in the ridge right over the hearth (two over a hearth that sits where two ridge pieces meet) and keep anything (a ridge log) out of the slot. The square smoke hole is for single-slope roofs. Hot reload no longer leaves turf pieces magenta.

0.1.0: First version. Turf roofs in all eight of the game's roof shapes (26° and 45°: straight, ridge, outer and inner corner), snapping and holding like the wooden roofs; torn, drooping edges where a side is open, clean ones against a neighbour; sticks in the sod; +1 comfort under turf. Smoke-hole turf roofs (26° and 45°) with a hooded vent the hearth's smoke rolls out of.
