Gary the Greydwarf is a purple forest companion: friendly, defensive, impossible to kill, and occasionally generous with snacks. Uses the game's native Greydwarf body, movement, weapons, and animation; no API key or external service required.

F3 summons one Gary per player, or recalls that same Gary beside you. Shift+F3 tells him to stay where he is; F3 resumes following. Both keys are configurable. Calls need clear dry ground nearby. He waits outside dungeons and for you to respawn; use F3 to call him after a portal trip.

You, other players, and Quad’s AICompanion characters can walk through Gary, so he cannot block doorways or push you around. Terrain/enemy collisions and petting stay active.

Gary follows quietly until a hostile creature actually damages his player or Gary himself. He defends against that attacker for up to 30 seconds and stays within a bounded defense radius. He can defend himself while waiting, including outside a dungeon or while his player is dead/offline, and resumes waiting afterward. He does not pick fights with nearby wildlife, players, or tamed friends. Friendly player/pet hits do not hurt him.

At 20% health, Gary abandons combat and tries to flee 40 metres from you. Damage cannot take his health below 5%, and native death checks are suppressed for Gary alone. He heals rapidly at a safe distance, slowly while escaping, and comes back at 90%. If his zone unloads, a saved three-minute recovery deadline lets him heal before being recalled. Calling him does not interrupt his retreat.

Gary gathers real raspberries, blueberries, mushrooms, and loose feathers into a saved six-item stash, then occasionally tosses one near your feet (roughly every 3–5 minutes). At most two pockets hold feathers; when food is available, feathers are a 20 percent gift choice. Empty pockets produce no gifts. Loose feathers must already be owned by his simulator and within two metres as he passes; player-dropped items, nearby players, bases, placed/custom items and Gary's own gifts are skipped. He skips nearby players/protected plants, never claims plant ownership, and preserves normal regrowth and resource scaling. Gifts land as normal ground items; both stash and cooldown survive F6/world saves.

Look at Gary and press your normal Use key (E) to pet him: a friendly pat, quiet forest chirp, purple sparkles, and a brief happy dance with no stat bonus. He greets you after absences/dungeons, celebrates foes he fought and your nearby victories over huge enemies, and occasionally chirps/looks toward nearby visible danger. Huge enemies make him nervous; warnings never start attacks.

When you settle near a burning fire or hearth, Gary finds clear ground beside it and relaxes. When you pause to build, he watches from the side, away from your placement ghost; walking away resumes following. His retreats choose reachable dry ground away from both you and danger, preferring cover. Combat and healing interrupt social activities. Campfire, building, reactions, and warnings have separate settings.

Within 90 metres, Gary can notice a loaded burial chamber, sunken crypt, troll cave, or frost cave entrance. He leads a little way ahead, waits if you lag behind, and gives up if the route is obstructed. Confirmed fully looted dungeons are skipped; unfinished dungeons can be revisited after a ten-minute shown-entrance cooldown. Counts unopened chest contents, loose treasure, cores, scrap piles, collectible cave materials, and removable valuables; ignores enemies, regrowing food, and ordinary furniture salvage. Reads saved chest item counts without opening/generating loot. Incomplete/unloaded scans stay unknown and eligible. Up to 512 cleared entrances are saved per player/world with your character; older visits need a complete scan. No distant locations/map pins; he waits outside.

Install Gary on the host/dedicated server and participating clients for multiplayer. The server authenticates commands and finds the existing saved Gary, including unloaded zones, before spawning. Creature simulation and gifts run only on its network owner. Uses tagged vanilla Greydwarf saves, so removing the mod leaves a normal tamed greydwarf instead of an unknown-prefab object. Special behavior and purple appearance require the mod.

Gary can ride your boat. While following within 12 metres and five metres of vertical reach, he hops to a clear deck spot beside you and stays there through sailing, turns and waves. F3 can call/recall him onto a free deck spot too. He does not take a player seat, control the boat, push its hull, or fight/forage while riding. After a brief grace period he hops onto nearby dry ground or a dock when you disembark. If you fall into the water, die, leave the area or tell him to wait, he stays aboard until you return or recall him. Injured passengers rest/heal aboard rather than fleeing into the ocean. Boarding state survives F6 and world saves; a missing/destroyed ship releases him alive. No transform parenting to the boat. Toggle Companion/RideBoats in F7.

Your Gary has a purple moving marker on the minimap and full map. If he unloads but his saved object is still known, it shows Gary (last seen) at that position; it disappears when no position is known. Only your Gary is shown. The marker is transient, does not become a saved/shared map pin, and cleans up on F6/world/player changes. Toggle Companion/MapMarker in F7.

Gary now has eight more playful habits. F8 throws one actual Wood from your inventory toward reachable ground about nine metres ahead; he chases it, carries the same normal item back and waits for a pat. Fetch is bounded to 16 metres/35 seconds, takes priority over crypt guiding and other idle activities, chases the ground landing while Wood is airborne, retries temporary navigation failures for up to five seconds, requires compatible ownership, and stops for combat, recovery, waiting, boats, portals, or F6; the real Wood stays in the world. There is no item escrow or conjured refund. A returned item can be picked up normally.

Nearby wave, cheer, dance, sit and relax emotes get an awkward Gary imitation. Shift+E on your own built wood pile marks a reachable leaf/branch nest beside it; repeat on that pile to forget it. When you pause nearby for eight seconds, he curls up there. His home reference survives saves/F6; the original pile retains normal materials, support and removal behavior. In rain he looks for a reachable nearby roof while you settle down and shakes dry afterward.

On boats he prefers a clear forward deck spot, watches ahead, chirps at visible seabirds and crouches when waves tilt or lift the hull strongly. He wears a small matte twig-and-feather crown, tiny pouch and ear feather in muted forest colors, using native game materials with emission disabled. He also greets your nearby Quad AICompanion characters, watches their building/gathering work and occasionally imitates their motions. Before a forest gift he briefly holds up a mesh-only preview, then tosses the actual stash item. These activities give no buffs or extra resources; combat/recovery take priority.

Personality activities and Appearance accessories have separate F7 toggles. Fetch key is configurable under Controls; nest marking uses Left Shift plus the normal Use key. Gary's native rig has no wave/sleep/crouch animations, so these use local cosmetic bone poses replicated from bounded activity state. New poses, accessories, nested roofs, boat geometry, F6 and ownership changes need in-game playtests.

Settings are in F7 → Mod settings, or BepInEx/config/com.bobisme.gary.cfg. Compiled and checked against native Linux assemblies, including food-conservation, injury, defense, petting, reaction and retreat policy tests. Appearance, pathfinding, audio, live multiplayer, and save/restart behavior need in-game playtests.

Reforestation: while you are near your bed, Gary now and then plants a real sapling on open wild ground nearby, beside a stump you left when he can, never in your base, near buildings, on farmland or paths. Seeds and cones he picks up let him plant more. Radius, interval and limit are in F7.

Caretaker: around home he stacks deadfall by his nest, gathers freshly dropped harvest for you, replants crops you harvest by hand (with your seeds) and stamps out stray fire. He listens to old trees, shows you berries he found, shouts Timber!, senses Omens' signs and skies, and has opinions about your trophies. Each job is in F7.
