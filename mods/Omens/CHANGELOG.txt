0.6.2: Its Claude Tools commands work with Claude Tools under its new id (com.quad.claudetools, from 1.2.2), and still with older copies.

0.6.1: Smoother play: Omens finds Claude Tools through BepInEx's plugin list instead of searching every loaded object every five seconds, which cost about 10 ms each time and lined up with other mods into a regular hitch.

0.6.0: Omens that return: a bad omen left to come to pass can come back half a day later, worse, near the home it struck (a cairn's dead as grave candles, a drained deer or gnawed carcass as a blood moon, the drowned man as his black ship, a dead troll as Thor's storm, an abandoned camp as a scorched circle), likelier the less the gods like you. The rune bones, carved at a workbench from 6 bone fragments and 2 resin: cast them to learn which way the nearest omen lies, roughly how far, whether it is good or bad, how the gods see you and what tonight holds. A new omen, the lightning-split oak, brings Thor's storm: eight minutes of thunder and rain with lightning striking near anyone in the open, each strike warned by a crackle on the ground; 20 coins buried at the oak stop the strikes. Offerings now also soften a blood moon or storm already raging.

0.5.0: The gods' favour: each world has a standing with the gods (-5 to +5) that everyone hears about when it changes. Answering omens raises it, an offering at a shrine raises it by two, letting an answerable warning come to pass lowers it, and robbing a cursed hoard lowers it by two. Favoured worlds see fewer bad omens and the beloved get bigger gifts; forsaken worlds see omens more often, with packs a level stronger. Three new omens: a forgotten shrine (leave 3 honey: rested, the land revealed, favour +2), a humming rune stone that brings a night of northern lights (a shimmering green sky, half the night creatures, faster healing and learning), and a ship with black sails offshore whose draugr crew comes ashore at night unless you light a 20-wood warning beacon, which sends it away into the mist. Most omens now have several readings.

0.4.0: Ten new omens, for seventeen in all. Bad: a gnawed carcass in the mountains (wolves raid unless you leave 3 raw meat), a Fuling war banner in the plains (Fulings raid unless you tear it down, which brings its three guards at once), a drowned man on the shore (draugr come ashore unless you pay his passage with 10 coins), a cursed hoard (take its coins and gems and the dead hunt you that night, wherever you are; leave it and nothing happens), unlit grave candles (ghosts unless relit with 3 resin), and a scorched circle (surtlings). Good: dancing lights that lead to a real treasure chest, a shed antler that brings a great two-star stag dropping a hard antler, a fallen star that leaves ore for the land, and a cloaked wanderer who vanishes as you approach and leaves everyone nearby learning every skill 50% faster for 20 minutes. After a response the sign stays 20 seconds so everyone sees it: the candles light, the banner falls, coins rest on the drowned man's eyes, the troll burns.

0.3.0: Blood Moon, the first omen that changes the night itself. A blood-soaked circle of stones (most land) foretells it: that night the sky turns red for everyone, and night creatures spawn twice as often, up to 1.5× as many at once, and level up twice as often. Leaving an offering of 4 raw meat at the circle softens it (1.5×, 1.25×, 1.5× and a paler sky) instead of averting it. It wanes at daybreak. Response costs are now looked up from item prefabs.

0.2.0: Three new omens. A scattered cairn (Meadows, Black Forest, Swamp) brings a skeleton raid unless its bones are laid to rest with 5 bone fragments. A drained deer (Meadows, Black Forest) sends a hunting pack, led by a stronger one and suited to the base's biome, at the nearest base at night once someone is home. Gulls over a shore strand real fish there when read. Omens are now chosen among those that fit the spot (ravens need open sky, gulls a shore), keeping the 60/40 split. The test command can place the new omens and list the game's raid events.

0.1.8: Everyone nearby sees a dead troll burn, not only the player who lit it: the host announces the burn and each game lights its own copy of the carcass.

0.1.7: Signs already in the world keep working after a hot reload (F6): they are rebuilt with the reloaded mod. Before, burning such a troll used the resin but never reached the host, so the carcass stayed.

0.1.6: The log says why a bad omen fizzled: raids turned off in the world, no workbench or bed in reach, kept waiting too long by another raid, or a missing raid type.

0.1.5: The abandoned camp reads as a camp: a bedroll beside a cold fire pit (no glowing coals), a skull, a ragged tunic, wooden shield and flint spear, bones and a log, without the glint that made everything look like loot. The test command can clear an omen.

0.1.4: The dead troll is easier to aim at: the box you use wraps the ragdoll's own bone shapes wherever it settles. Burning it lights the fire on the carcass, not where it was placed.

0.1.3: The dead troll can be burned again: the box you aim at follows the carcass when its ragdoll slides downhill, instead of staying where it was placed. The omen test command can avert an omen.

0.1.2: Ravens appear only where the sky above them is open (clearings, shores, open ground), so they can be seen; a spot under dense forest is skipped for another. A blessing's ravens keep circling for two minutes after it is read instead of vanishing at once, and now really fly beak-first around their circle (0.1.1 turned them by the sign's random yaw).

0.1.1: Circling crows fly above the treetops (they were hidden inside Black Forest canopy), at 1.5× size, facing along their circle and banking into the turn, without a second perched copy. With Claude Tools installed, an omen command places, lists and fulfils omens on the host for testing.

0.1.0: First Omens. The host places a sign near a player every 1.5 in-game days on average, 60% bad. A dead troll (Black Forest) brings a troll raid on the nearest base that night unless burned with 5 resin; an abandoned camp (Meadows/Black Forest) brings a greydwarf raid; circling ravens rest nearby players and reveal 250 metres of map. Signs are discovered by walking near them, with an on-screen reading, a chat line for everyone and a map pin. Unseen signs fade after a day. Raids use the game's own events and respect a world with raids turned off.
