0.1.8: Everyone nearby sees a dead troll burn, not only the player who lit it: the host announces the burn and each game lights its own copy of the carcass.

0.1.7: Signs already in the world keep working after a hot reload (F6): they are rebuilt with the reloaded mod. Before, burning such a troll used the resin but never reached the host, so the carcass stayed.

0.1.6: The log says why a bad omen fizzled: raids turned off in the world, no workbench or bed in reach, kept waiting too long by another raid, or a missing raid type.

0.1.5: The abandoned camp reads as a camp: a bedroll beside a cold fire pit (no glowing coals), a skull, a ragged tunic, wooden shield and flint spear, bones and a log, without the glint that made everything look like loot. The test command can clear an omen.

0.1.4: The dead troll is easier to aim at: the box you use wraps the ragdoll's own bone shapes wherever it settles. Burning it lights the fire on the carcass, not where it was placed.

0.1.3: The dead troll can be burned again: the box you aim at follows the carcass when its ragdoll slides downhill, instead of staying where it was placed. The omen test command can avert an omen.

0.1.2: Ravens appear only where the sky above them is open (clearings, shores, open ground), so they can be seen; a spot under dense forest is skipped for another. A blessing's ravens keep circling for two minutes after it is read instead of vanishing at once, and now really fly beak-first around their circle (0.1.1 turned them by the sign's random yaw).

0.1.1: Circling crows fly above the treetops (they were hidden inside Black Forest canopy), at 1.5× size, facing along their circle and banking into the turn, without a second perched copy. With Claude Tools installed, an omen command places, lists and fulfils omens on the host for testing.

0.1.0: First Omens. The host places a sign near a player every 1.5 in-game days on average, 60% bad. A dead troll (Black Forest) brings a troll raid on the nearest base that night unless burned with 5 resin; an abandoned camp (Meadows/Black Forest) brings a greydwarf raid; circling ravens rest nearby players and reveal 250 metres of map. Signs are discovered by walking near them, with an on-screen reading, a chat line for everyone and a map pin. Unseen signs fade after a day. Raids use the game's own events and respect a world with raids turned off.
