Signs appear in the world that foretell good or bad things, and what they foretell really happens.

Every 1.5 in-game days or so, the host places an omen 45 to 85 metres from a player, away from buildings. Walk near it to read it: a message appears on screen for players nearby, a chat line for everyone, and a pin on your map. An omen nobody finds fades after a day without effect.

Dead troll (Black Forest, bad): "The forest is angry." That night, trolls raid the nearest base. Burn the carcass first (use it, with 5 resin) to avert the raid.

Scattered cairn (Meadows, Black Forest, Swamp, bad): stones and bones kicked apart. That night, skeletons raid the nearest base, unless you lay the bones to rest (use it, with 5 bone fragments).

Abandoned camp (Meadows and Black Forest, bad): a bedroll by a cold fire, a skull, and dropped belongings. That night, greydwarfs raid the nearest base.

Drained deer (Meadows and Black Forest, bad): "Something hunts in the night." At night, once someone is home, a hunting pack led by a stronger creature comes for the nearest base: greydwarfs, draugr, wolves, goblins or seekers, depending on where the base stands.

Blood-soaked circle (most land, bad): "The moon will bleed tonight." That night the sky turns red for everyone, and night creatures spawn twice as often, more at once and stronger. Leave an offering of 4 raw meat at the circle to soften it; it cannot be averted. It wanes at daybreak.

Circling ravens (open sky, good): "Odin is watching." Players nearby become rested, and the land within 250 metres is revealed on their maps.

Gulls over the shore (good): "The sea is generous." Real fish are stranded on the shore for the taking.

About 60% of omens are bad, chosen among those that fit the spot. A bad omen comes at nightfall when seen by day, or a few minutes later when seen at night, to the nearest workbench or bed within 1,500 metres of the sign. Raids are the game's own, with their usual messages and music, and never interrupt one already running. A world with raids turned off gets only good omens. Signs are made from the game's own models and cannot be mined, looted or built on.

Install on the host and every player. The host decides everything and keeps a small record per world in BepInEx/config/omens. Frequency, the bad chance, how long signs wait, raid reach and each omen can be changed in F7.
