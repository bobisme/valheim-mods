A colony of the reborn, built on Valheim's own systems. Raise the High-seat pillars, take in the fallen Odin sends, give them beds, work and gear, keep them fed and happy, defend them, and send the worthy to Valhalla on a funeral pyre.

Founding: High-seat pillars (hammer, Misc; 2 fine wood, 4 wood, 1 resin, near a workbench) found a colony 60 m across (more as it grows); colonies stand 300 m apart. Use the pillars to hold council: the colony window shows the hall, its people, rooms, work, the Thing, the gods and the saga.

Odin's attention (renown) is wealth (what you've built, coins and valuables in its chests), comfort (the beds' comfort, the game's own) and deeds (raids held, feasts, souls sent to Valhalla). Once renown reaches 20 (a quarter more for each newcomer after) and a bed is free, Odin may send someone at dawn: Hugin says who comes, and at dusk a Valkyrie sets them down at the pillars. Take them in or turn them away from the colony window. Each soul has an Old Norse name, two traits, a profession they'd like and the story of how they fell; souls who fell together arrive together and like each other.

Villagers live as players do: they eat (three meals, from their bag or the colony's chests; health follows food), sleep in their own bed (or by the hall fire, unhappily), go to the great hall at dusk to sit, eat and talk, and shelter from heavy rain. They fight with what you give them (melee, shields, bows), flee when hurt, and when they fall they leave a tombstone with their bag and rise again at their bed. One with no bed, or disgraced, is lost for good and rises as a named draugr carrying their hoard.

Professions: hauler, woodcutter, forager, farmer (harvests and replants with seeds from the stores), guard, miner, hunter, fisherman, smelter (feeds smelters and kilns through their own controls), cook, skald and more. Work comes from a job board: zones you mark (woodlot, quarry, fields, foraging and hunting grounds, fishing dock, guard post), things lying in the colony, fires and stations running low. Jobs are leases, so two villagers (or two players' games) never take the same one; the board says why a job is blocked ("no pickaxe of tier 2"). Gear matters: an axe's tier, durability that wears.

Rooms: with the hammer's "Mark rooms and zones" chosen, click a room's corners on the floor, click the first again, and choose what it is. Rooms are checked the game's way (walls round the edge, roof over the floor, a door, what's inside) and graded D to S; the overlay shows rooms, zones and the gaps in walls. Longhouse, great hall, stockpile, smithy, Odin's shrine, temples, healer's house, treasure room, burial ground, watchtower. Ranks from camp to hold need people and rooms, and raise the colony's cap and radius.

Mood: seven needs (food, rest, shelter, company, safety, faith, beauty) and moodlets (a fine meal, a feast, the hall, an own bed, unpaid wages, a friend lost...) make a mood from -100 to +100 that sets how fast they work (60% to 130%); the panel says why. Miserable villagers slack, brawl and steal, and the disgraced may desert.

The gold goes somewhere: wages every five days from the treasury chest (all or nothing; unpaid villagers grumble), feasts in the great hall, offerings at the gods' altars and coins thrown into a sacred bog for favour without end. Gilded high seat and banners make halls grand. The Thing meets every five days with petitions drawn from what really happened. Worthy villagers (100 worth from kills, work and feasts) can be sent to Valhalla on a funeral pyre at dusk with grave goods: deeds, Odin's favour and a guaranteed newcomer.

Raids at a colony sound the war horn: fighters take their posts, the rest run for the great hall. Raids grow with renown. Works with Omens (Odin's favour is the gods' Fate), AICompanion (claims respected both ways), Gary (villagers pet him), SkalTavern; needs none of them.

Install on the host and every player: a host without Jarldom deletes the pillars and villagers when their area loads (the hammer refuses Jarldom pieces where the host lacks it). Before uninstalling, disband the colony from its window: everyone's gear goes into chests by the pillars. Claude Tools adds the jarldom command (status, roster, villager, rooms, board, log, and test commands for single player and hosts).
