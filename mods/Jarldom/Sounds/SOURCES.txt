The great hall's crowd loops: recordings from freesound.org, all Creative Commons 0 (public domain, no attribution required).
Each was cut to a seamless loop (its end crossfaded into its start), made mono 22050 Hz and evened in loudness.

hall_small.ogg   Breviceps, "Busy Room Ambience / Small crowd / People talking in background"  https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/457043/
hall_big.ogg     Breviceps, "Busy Room Ambience / People talking in background #2"             https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/465699/
hall_bar.ogg     EpicWizard, "Bar Crowd in Belgrade"                                           https://freesound.org/people/EpicWizard/sounds/394290/
hall_walla.ogg   IENBA, "Small Crowd Walla" (4 s cut from each end)                             https://freesound.org/people/IENBA/sounds/653920/

The skald's songs: made by Bob for Jarldom, each in a man's and a woman's voice (the skald's own). Cut to mono 44100 Hz, evened in loudness (-16 LUFS).

song_drekkum_f.ogg     "Drekkum, drengir!" (a woman skald)
