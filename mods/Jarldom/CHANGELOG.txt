0.1.1: The founder can let other players run the colony with them (the hall tab: a button for each player near it; only the founder chooses): they mark rooms, set trades and call the Thing. Ctrl+Shift+click on a room's floor with the room marker finds the room from its walls in one go (a red post shows where the walls leak). Saved data unchanged: 0.1.0 and 0.1.1 can share a server.
