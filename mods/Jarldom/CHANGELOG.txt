0.1.0: First version. Found a colony with the high-seat pillars and grow it from camp to hold: newcomers sent by Odin, villagers who eat, sleep, work, fight, rise again and can be lost; trades from hauler to skald on a job board fed by the zones and rooms you mark; mood, wages, feasts, the gods and their temples, the funeral pyre to Valhalla; the Thing, held in the great hall when you call it; raids, the Fulings' tribute, captives, a draugr jarl's warbands and the Forsaken-touched; clothes woven from flax; ore claims and mining camps in the wilds, iron from sunken crypts; trade runs on foot, by cart and by ship; following the jarl on expeditions; outposts and carters; builders who mend, rebuild and pave. Built for multiplayer: villager state on still objects so walking villagers send little, and games on different versions don't write each other's colonies.
