0.1.6: No more stutter every 8 seconds near a colony with many chests: the work scan read every chest in the colony over again for each thing it checked (166 times a scan, about 55 ms); it now counts the stores once and asks the count.

New test command: jarldom icon <prefab> [0|1|current].

0.1.4: The rooms and zones drawn with the hammer out go away when it's put away (they stayed up, and with the hammer out they were redrawn every second). New test command: jarldom overlay.

0.1.3: Less log spam and less work with the hammer out: the rooms and zones drawn on the ground are redrawn only when a room or zone changes (they were redrawn with every save of the colony, a few times a minute, each time logging a font warning per label). New test commands: jarldom env <weather|off> and jarldom fuel.

0.1.2: Finding a room with Ctrl+Shift+click keeps to the floor under its roof (a room whose walls have small gaps is still found), and a room that nearly fills its box comes out as a clean rectangle: tried on a colony's eleven marked rooms, it matched the hand-marked outlines within 10 cm.
